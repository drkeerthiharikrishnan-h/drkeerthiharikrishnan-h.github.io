    Data and AI engineers: Know the data by department and modality

    Units, conditions, dictionary versions and the joins that make a number comparable

    An IC50 depends on the assay, so store its conditions with it

    • Competitive inhibitor: Ki = IC50 / (1 + [S]/Km)
    • Higher substrate raises IC50: 2× Ki at Km, 101× at 100×Km
    • Never pool IC50 and Ki from different formats into one label

    Source: Kalliokoski, Kramer, Vulpetti and Gedeck (PLOS ONE), 2013; Knight, Feldman, Balla, Balla and Shokat (Nature Protocols), 2007

    Speaker notes

    Worked from the equation, the same molecule reads about 50-fold apart between [S] = Km and [S] = 100 x Km (2 x Ki versus 101 x Ki). The example table keeps ATP concentration and assay version on every row [D:sm-potency]. Ask the room: which column in your training set records the substrate concentration?

    Censored values and DMPK units each need their own column

    • "Greater than 30,000 nM" gets no pChEMBL value
    • CL_int per mg protein, per million cells or scaled per kg
    • A logD without its pH cannot be compared

    Source: Scientific Data (Nature Portfolio), 2024; Acta Pharmaceutica, 2019; Journal of Cheminformatics, 2023

    Speaker notes

    ChEMBL computes pChEMBL only when the relation is equals and the unit is nM. Imatinib alone has 105 IC50 records with a greater-than relation. In the messy DMPK table a scaled clearance sits in the in vitro CL_int column [D:sm-dmpk]. Ask: how does your loader treat a value that arrives with a relation symbol, or a unit that does not match its matrix?

    A biologic's number carries its method, so the method is part of the key

    • KD = koff/kon; surface avidity can inflate affinity
    • ADC DAR: record the method; an unrecorded one is the defect
    • Plasma stability rows need species and timepoint

    Source: mAbs (Taylor & Francis), 2023; Scientific Reports, 2014; mAbs (Taylor & Francis), 2020; American Chemical Society, 2016

    Speaker notes

    One study found native SEC-MS and HIC statistically equivalent for average DAR across several cysteine-linked ADCs. So the defect is a DAR with no method, not a clash between good methods [D:adc-dar]. One study of site-specific ADCs measured DAR stability in plasma in vitro; a 72 h and a 168 h value are different measurements [D:adc-dar]. Ask: which column tells you the DAR method in your conjugate table?

    CAR-T release values need their denominators before lots can be ranked

    • Report VCN per CAR-positive cell, not per µg of DNA
    • Specific lysis needs its effector-to-target ratio
    • Target-cell antigen density: bead-calibrated, not raw MFI

    Source: US FDA (CBER), 2024; Ikoma (Revista Brasileira de Hematologia e Hemoterapia), 2017; Poncelet, Lecompte, Bauters and Mullier (MDPI IJMS), 2026

    Speaker notes

    FDA recommends the average VCN per CAR-positive cell, calculated from VCN per cell and the CAR-positive percentage. For CT-LOT-2024-009 that is 3.1 / 0.520 = 5.96, and the three lots tested at 5:1 can be ranked while the 1:1 lot cannot [D:cart-release]. Ask: what would a model learn if E:T ratio were missing from half the rows?

    An AAV titre is a method result, and only one per lot is reportable

    • ddPCR counts copies; qPCR reads a standard curve
    • Store method, target region and qPCR standard
    • Titre is vg/mL; dose in vg/kg lives elsewhere

    Source: Lock et al. (PubMed 24328707), 2014

    Speaker notes

    In one comparison, ddPCR gave up to 4-fold higher titres than a standard qPCR for single-stranded genomes. In the messy table one lot has two reportable titres, 2.1E+13 and 2.6E+13 vg/mL; dosing from the higher one would give 81% of the intended genomes if the lower is right [D:gt-vector-titre]. Ask: where would your schema store a reference standard id?

    An RNA medicine's identity is its sequence plus its chemistry, and its lots form a chain

    • Oligonucleotide: sequence plus modification map; purity as % full-length
    • mRNA-LNP: integrity, encapsulation and size, each with its method
    • Every drug-product lot links to its mRNA drug-substance lot

    Source: European Medicines Agency, 2017; European Medicines Agency, 2021

    Speaker notes

    Nusinersen has 18 nucleotides with 2'-MOE sugars and 17 phosphorothioate linkages, a mixture of up to 2^17 diastereoisomers held constant from batch to batch. In the EMA assessment of Comirnaty, RNA integrity fell in the first batches of a new drug-substance process and rose after nucleotide volumes were adjusted. In the messy example, 0.94 in a percent column drags the mean encapsulation from 93.0% to 46.5% [D:rna-lnp-release]. Ask: which column in your release table links a drug-product lot to its drug-substance lot?

    Clinical data trace from collection to analysis on one subject id

    • CDASH collects, SDTM organises, ADaM analyses
    • Define-XML describes every dataset; FDA and PMDA require it
    • USUBJID must be identical across SDTM and ADaM

    Source: CDISC, 2023; CDISC, 2024; CDISC, 2021; CDISC; US FDA (CDER, CBER), 2026

    Speaker notes

    An improper USUBJID is a common error that often forces resubmission. AETERM holds the verbatim text and AEDECOD the MedDRA term. LOINC codes appear in LBLOINC when the laboratory provides them. Ask: where in your pipeline could a site prefix be dropped from a subject id?

    A safety case is valid only with four elements and a coded, versioned term

    • Reporter, patient, suspect reaction, suspect product
    • EU rules: serious cases due within 15 days of receipt
    • MedDRA releases each March and September

    Source: European Medicines Agency and Heads of Medicines Agencies, 2017; MedDRA MSSO, 2009; European Medicines Agency, 2020

    Speaker notes

    Under EU rules non-serious cases are due within 90 days. EudraVigilance has required ICH E2B(R3)-based reporting since 30 June 2022. Organisations should document the MedDRA version used and be aware of how each type of change can affect data output. Ask: if you count preferred terms across two years, how many MedDRA versions are in your data?

    Manufacturing results are judged against a versioned specification on the lot

    • Each test links to a procedure and acceptance criterion
    • An out-of-specification result is investigated, not overwritten
    • Keep the failing first run and the retest as two rows

    Source: European Medicines Agency (ICH), 2000; European Medicines Agency (ICH), 1999; European Medicines Agency (ICH), 2003; US FDA, 2022

    Speaker notes

    In the messy example, water at 0.62% against a limit of 0.5% carries no OOS flag [D:cmc-batch]. The good table keeps run 1 at 93.8% and the retest at 101.0% under one investigation id [D:cmc-batch]. Ask: does your schema have room for both results?

    Real-world data become usable only after mapping to standard concepts

    • OMOP CDM: one structure, standardised vocabularies
    • Local source concepts sit above 2,000,000,000, never standard
    • DARWIN EU partners convert their data to OMOP

    Source: OHDSI; OHDSI CDM Working Group; OHDSI CDM Working Group; European Medicines Agency

    Speaker notes

    Keep the source code in condition_source_value so the mapping can be checked. DARWIN EU reports about 40 data partners and about 250 million patients (EMA key figures, 2026). Ask: what share of your source codes map to a standard concept?

    Join internal compounds and targets to public records on structure and stable ids

    • InChIKey: salt and parent keys differ in the first block
    • CHEMBL1642 (mesylate) points to parent CHEMBL941
    • Strip Ensembl version suffix for joins; keep it for provenance

    Source: InChI Trust (IUPAC); NCBI PubChem; EMBL-EBI ChEMBL; UniProt Consortium; Open Targets

    Speaker notes

    P00519 is human ABL1 and P00520 is mouse ABL1, with the same protein name. Trials join on the NCT number, NCT plus 8 digits, while the sponsor's protocol number sits in its own field. Ask: which of your keys would silently join to the wrong species?

    A clean table becomes a model input only with a context of use and honest splits

    • State the question and decision the model informs
    • Scaffold splits test harder than random splits
    • On imbalanced labels, read precision-recall, not only ROC

    Source: US FDA, 2025; European Medicines Agency and US FDA (joint), 2026; Wu et al. (Royal Society of Chemistry), 2018; Saito and Rehmsmeier (PLoS ONE), 2015

    Speaker notes

    The FDA's January 2025 draft is non-binding and leaves drug discovery uses out of scope. The January 2026 FDA-EMA principles for AI in drug development ask for a clear context of use and documented data governance; the 2021 GMLP principles were written for medical devices. Ask: is your test set split by scaffold or at random?

    Learn

    Small molecules: potency is a number plus its conditions

    A small molecule is a chemically made drug. Its discovery data live in an assay database: one row per compound, batch, assay and result. The most common result is the IC50, the concentration of compound that halves the activity of the target in one assay. The IC50 is not a property of the compound alone. For a competitive inhibitor, the inhibition constant Ki equals IC50 divided by (1 + [S]/Km), where [S] is the substrate concentration in the well and Km is the substrate concentration at half the enzyme’s top speed[1, 2]. So the same compound gives a higher IC50 when the assay uses more substrate.

    A worked example shows the size of the effect. Suppose a kinase assay runs ATP (the substrate) at its Km, so [S]/Km is 1 and the IC50 is twice the Ki. Run the same compound at 100 times Km and the IC50 becomes 101 times the Ki[1, 3]. That is a 50-fold change in the reported number with no change in the molecule. This is why the example potency table stores the ATP concentration, the assay id and the assay version on every row[data]. A careful team records the format and does not pool IC50 and Ki values blindly into one training label. A 2013 analysis by Novartis scientists found that mixing public IC50 values from different assays added a moderate amount of noise, and that Ki values could be added after correcting by an offset[1].

    The beginner module covers pIC50, log-scale averaging of replicates and the pIC50-in-the-IC50-column defect; this module assumes them[data].

    Not every row is a measured value. ChEMBL, the public bioactivity database run by EMBL-EBI, stores a relation symbol (greater than, less than, equals) in its own field next to the value[4]. ChEMBL computes its pChEMBL potency only when the relation is “equals”, the unit is nM and the type is an IC50, EC50, Ki, Kd or a similar dose-response type[5]. For imatinib, 105 IC50 records carry a “greater than” relation, such as greater than 30,000 nM, with no pChEMBL value[6]. A single-concentration result, such as 42% inhibition at 10 µM, is a different result type and cannot be turned into an IC50[data].

    DMPK (drug metabolism and pharmacokinetics) results join to potency on compound and batch[data]. Two of them need their conditions. LogD, a measure of how much a compound prefers fat to water, depends on pH, so a logD without its pH cannot be compared[7]. Intrinsic clearance (CLint) from liver microsomes is reported in µL/min/mg protein, from hepatocytes in µL/min per million cells, and a scaled whole-body clearance in mL/min/kg; the three are not interchangeable[8]. Scaling a microsomal value to mL/min/kg needs stated factors, such as mg of microsomal protein per g of liver and g of liver per kg of body weight, so a scaled value cannot be turned back without them[data].

    Before any IC50 exists, a high-throughput screen (HTS) tests many compounds once, plate by plate. Each plate is only as good as its controls. The Z-factor, defined by Zhang and colleagues, is a single number that combines the gap between the control signals and their spread[9]. Its control-based form is Z’ = 1 minus 3 times the sum of the two control standard deviations, divided by the difference of the two control means; values from 0.5 to 1 are classed as excellent[10, 11]. The Assay Guidance Manual’s plate criteria ask for Z’ of at least 0.4, or a signal window of at least 2, and treat drift or edge effects under 20% as insignificant[12]. The example screen uses a stricter rule, Z’ at least 0.5[data]. So a plate table needs the control means and standard deviations in signal units, an edge or drift result, and a plate status set by a written rule. A Z computed from sample wells is a different number and belongs in its own column[data].

    Biologics and gene therapy: antibodies, ADCs, CAR-T cells and AAV vectors

    An antibody (class) that blocks a receptor (mechanism) is a protein made in living cells. Its binding is often measured by surface plasmon resonance (SPR), which reports an association rate (kon, in per molar per second), a dissociation rate (koff, per second) and the affinity KD, their ratio[13]. A worked example: kon of 1 × 10^5 per molar per second and koff of 1 × 10^-4 per second give a KD of 1 × 10^-9 M, or 1 nM[13]. The assay format is part of the number. A two-armed antibody binding two antigens on a surface can look far stronger than it is, an effect called avidity[14]. The example binding table therefore keeps antigen form and method beside every KD[data].

    An antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) is an antibody (class) that carries a toxic payload into a target cell (mechanism). Its key release number is the average DAR, the drug-to-antibody ratio. One study compared native size-exclusion chromatography with mass spectrometry (nSEC-MS) and hydrophobic interaction chromatography (HIC) across several cysteine-linked ADCs and found the two statistically equivalent for average DAR[15]. So the defect to catch is not a disagreement between good methods. It is a DAR with no recorded method, because then nobody can tell whether the value is the release result[data]. Conjugation through lysine residues gives a wide spread of DARs in one batch[16]. One published study of site-specific ADCs measured DAR stability in plasma in vitro[17]. Store species and timepoint with every stability value, because a 72 h and a 168 h value are different measurements[data]. A conjugate lot traces back to its antibody lot, antibody clone and drug-linker lot, and that is the join a careful team keeps[data].

    A CAR-T product is a cell therapy (class) in which a patient’s own T cells are engineered to kill cells carrying a target antigen (mechanism). The FDA’s 2024 guidance covers its manufacturing, analytical testing and comparability[18]. Three numbers trip up newcomers. Vector copy number (VCN) counts integrated vector copies. FDA recommends reporting the average VCN per CAR-positive cell, which can be calculated from VCN per cell and the percentage of CAR-positive cells[18]. A value per microgram of DNA is a third denominator and fits neither column[data]. Specific lysis (the percentage of target cells killed) means nothing without its effector-to-target (E:T) ratio[data]. Antigen density belongs to the target cells used in the potency assay, not to the CAR-T lot[data]. Reported as mean fluorescence intensity (MFI) it is in arbitrary units, not molecules per cell: comparing MFI needs standardisation between instruments, and calibration beads convert it to antibodies bound per cell[19, 20]. Release testing also covers safety tests such as sterility and mycoplasma[18]. Each lot must trace to its donor, because the product is made for one patient[data]. The example CAR-T lots carry their own CT-LOT ids, so they cannot collide with the lot numbers of a tablet product[data].

    An AAV gene therapy is a gene therapy (class) that delivers a working gene inside a modified adeno-associated virus shell (mechanism). Its key release number is the vector genome titre, in vector genomes per mL. Quantitative PCR (qPCR) reads the titre off a standard curve; droplet digital PCR (ddPCR) counts DNA copies directly, with no standard curve[21]. In one comparison, ddPCR gave up to 4-fold higher titres than a standard qPCR for single-stranded genomes[21]. The gap can run the other way: in another study qPCR titres for five samples averaged 64 times ddPCR; with the standard’s concentration re-measured the gap fell to 5.5 times, and nine other samples run against a ddPCR-measured standard read 11% higher[22]. So method, amplified region and, for qPCR, the reference standard are part of the titre, and only one titre per lot is reportable[data]. A titre in vg/mL is not a dose in vg/kg; the dose is worked out from the titre for a named patient weight[data].

    RNA medicines and vaccines: oligonucleotides and mRNA in lipid nanoparticles

    An antisense oligonucleotide (class) is a short, chemically made strand that binds a target RNA and changes how it is processed (mechanism); nusinersen changes the splicing of the SMN2 pre-mRNA[23]. Nusinersen has 18 nucleotides; every sugar carries a 2’-O-(2-methoxyethyl) (2’-MOE) group, and its 17 linkages are phosphorothioates[23]. Each phosphorothioate linkage forms in two spatial arrangements (stereoisomers), so the drug substance is a mixture of up to 2^17 diastereoisomers; EMA’s assessment records that the mixture stays the same from batch to batch[23]. So the identity of an oligonucleotide is its sequence plus its modification map (the beginner module’s rule). A sequence id alone names a different molecule as soon as the chemistry changes[data]. The release specification measures purity as the share of full-length product (n) by ion-pair HPLC with mass spectrometry, lists oligonucleotide impurities separately, and confirms identity by sequencing[23].

    An mRNA vaccine (class) delivers an mRNA that cells translate into an antigen; for Comirnaty it encodes the SARS-CoV-2 spike protein, packed in lipid nanoparticles (LNPs) that protect it from degradation (mechanism)[24]. Its records come in two linked lots. The mRNA drug substance is tested for, among other things, RNA integrity by capillary gel electrophoresis, the 5’ cap by RP-HPLC, the poly(A) tail by ddPCR and double-stranded RNA by immunoblot[24]. The drug product adds LNP size and polydispersity, RNA encapsulation and RNA content by fluorescence assays, lipid contents, potency by in vitro expression, and RNA integrity again[24]. Integrity is the share of full-length RNA, read from the main peak of the test[24].

    Integrity moves with the process. EMA’s assessors saw lower RNA integrity in the first batches of a new drug-substance process (Process 2); it rose again after the ATP and CTP volumes were adjusted[24]. A data engineer can only see a shift like that if every drug-product lot links to its drug-substance lot and every result carries its method. Store encapsulation on one scale, as a percentage with its method; a fraction stored in a percent column drags the mean far down[data]. The example values are illustrative, not product data.

    Discovery platforms: CRISPR screens and chemical probes

    A pooled CRISPR knockout screen switches off thousands of genes, one per cell, and counts which guide RNAs grow or vanish. MAGeCK, a published method, ranks guides and genes from read counts, compares treatment with control and controls the false discovery rate (FDR)[25]. A log2 fold change (LFC) on its own is not a hit; the row needs its FDR, its control sample and its timepoint[data]. Gene symbols change over time, so store the HGNC id, the stable identifier the HUGO Gene Nomenclature Committee assigns with each approved symbol[26].

    A chemical probe is a small molecule (class) used to switch off one protein in an experiment (mechanism), not a drug. For epigenetic targets, one published set of minimum criteria asks for potency under 100 nM, more than 30-fold selectivity within the protein family, profiling against an off-target panel, cell activity under 1 µM, and a close inactive analogue as a negative control; probes for other targets need criteria suited to them[27]. The selectivity fold only means something with the panel name and version beside it[data]. Recombinant protein batches have the same pattern: titer and endotoxin each arrive with their own unit column[data].

    Department data: clinical trials and safety

    Clinical data move through three CDISC standards. CDASH sets a standard way to collect data across studies[28]. SDTM organises the collected data into domains, such as AE for adverse events, for submission to the FDA and Japan’s PMDA[29]. ADaM builds analysis datasets that must trace back to SDTM[30]. Define-XML is the metadata file that describes each dataset and variable, and the FDA and PMDA require it for every study[31].

    The join key is USUBJID, the unique subject identifier. The FDA expects it to be unique per subject across all studies in an application and identical across SDTM and ADaM; an improper USUBJID is a common error that often forces a resubmission[32]. In the AE domain, AETERM holds the investigator’s verbatim text, AEDECOD the MedDRA preferred term, and AESTDTC the start date in ISO 8601 format[33]. Dictionaries are set per domain: MedDRA for adverse events, WHODrug Global for concomitant medicines, and LOINC codes in LBLOINC when the laboratory provides them[32].

    Pharmacovigilance (PV) is the work of detecting, assessing and preventing side effects of medicines[34]. Its unit of data is the individual case safety report (ICSR). A valid ICSR needs four elements: an identifiable reporter, a single identifiable patient, a suspected adverse reaction and a suspect medicinal product[35]. Under EU rules, serious cases are due within 15 days of receipt and non-serious ones within 90 days[35]. Since 30 June 2022, EudraVigilance reports must use the ISO ICSR format based on ICH E2B(R3)[36].

    MedDRA, the medical dictionary for regulatory activities, has five levels from 27 system organ classes down to lowest level terms[37]. As the MedDRA maintenance organisation described it in 2009, it is released twice a year: a March release that can change every level and a September release that changes only the two lowest levels[38]. Organisations should be aware of the types of change for their possible impact on data output, and document the version they used[39]. So a case id alone is not a complete key for counting: the count of a preferred term depends on the MedDRA version the case was coded in.

    Department data: manufacturing, real-world data and regulatory

    In manufacturing, the batch record and the release results share a lot number. A specification is a list of tests, each linked to an analytical procedure and an acceptance criterion[40, 41]. Stability studies follow ICH Q1A(R2), which sets how a product is stored and tested over time[42]. A result outside its acceptance criterion is out of specification (OOS) and must be investigated; a passing retest does not erase the first result[43]. The messy batch table shows both failures: a water result of 0.62% against a limit of not more than 0.5% with no OOS flag, and a failing assay value overwritten by its retest[data]. The good table keeps the failing first run (93.8%) and the passing retest (101.0%) as two rows linked to one investigation id, so the schema needs room for both[data]. The same lot number joins release results to stability pulls.

    Real-world data (RWD) are routinely collected health data, from sources such as health records, claims and registries[44]. The OMOP Common Data Model, run by the OHDSI community, gives them one structure and one set of vocabularies[45]. Each source code is mapped to a Standard Concept, and only Standard Concepts go into fields ending in _CONCEPT_ID[46]. The condition table keeps the original code in condition_source_value next to the mapped concept[47]. An unmapped row carries concept id 0 (see the beginner module)[data]. Local source concepts sit above 2,000,000,000 (see the beginner module); the example’s 2000000341 is one[48][data]. DARWIN EU, the EMA’s real-world evidence network, has about 40 data partners covering about 250 million patients (EMA key figures, 2026), and each partner converts its data to OMOP[49].

    Regulatory data have two skeletons. The Common Technical Document (CTD) has five modules: Module 1 is regional, Modules 2 to 5 are common to all regions[50]. IDMP is a set of five ISO standards for identifying medicinal products and substances, which the EMA runs through its SPOR data services[51]. In US study data, the trial summary links to one active moiety through its UNII code[32].

    Data wrangling as a discipline

    Tidy data is the starting shape: each variable a column, each observation a row, each type of observational unit a table[52]. In this field that means the unit, the relation symbol, the condition (pH, E:T ratio, ATP concentration) and the method version are columns, not text inside a value cell. Unit normalisation is arithmetic with a paper trail: micro is 10^-6 and nano 10^-9, so 0.48 µM is 480 nM[53].

    The rule a careful team never breaks is this: keep the source value and add the cleaned value beside it. ChEMBL works this way for some common activity types, such as IC50: its database downloads keep the original value, relation and units and add standardised fields for comparison[54]. Regulators describe the same idea as ALCOA++, the ten attributes listed in the beginner module; the last one, traceable, is the one a cleaning step most often breaks[55]. Part 11 asks for secure, time-stamped audit trails that record changes without hiding the earlier entry[56].

    Three more habits follow. Replicates are not duplicates: record a replicate id, or one summary row with its count, otherwise a join doubles the rows[data]. Dictionary versions are data: MedDRA guidance says to document the version used, so we store the MedDRA or OMOP vocabulary release with the coded data[39, 46]. Provenance columns (source system, extract date, mapping version) help a dataset stay findable and reusable later, which is the aim of the FAIR principles[57].

    Joining internal records to public sources

    Internal systems use internal ids; public databases use their own. The bridge for small molecules is the InChIKey, a fixed 27-character hash of the structure. Its first 14-character block encodes the skeleton; the second block encodes stereochemistry and isotopes[58]. PubChem’s PUG-REST service accepts an InChIKey and returns the compound id (CID)[59].

    Worked join. The example registry holds CPD-0101 as two batches, a free base and a mesylate salt[data]. The parent InChIKey KTUFNOKKBVMGRW-UHFFFAOYSA-N resolves to PubChem CID 5291 and ChEMBL CHEMBL941, imatinib[60, 6]. The salt key YLMAHDNUQAMNNX-UHFFFAOYSA-N differs in the first block, because the added methanesulfonic acid changes the connectivity; the second block and the final character are the same, because neither form has stereochemistry[61, 60, 58]. The salt key lands on CID 123596 and CHEMBL1642[61, 62]. ChEMBL’s molecule hierarchy names CHEMBL941 as the parent of CHEMBL1642, so strip the salt, or follow the hierarchy, before joining activity data[62]. Storing only the first block is worse: it cannot tell stereoisomers apart[58].

    Targets join through UniProt accessions, 6 or 10 characters such as P00519[63]. P00519 is human ABL1; P00520 is mouse ABL1 with the same protein name[64, 65]. Open Targets keys targets by Ensembl gene id[66], so ENSG00000097007 returns ABL1[67]. Watch the version suffix. UniProt lists EGFR’s gene as ENSG00000146648.22, while Open Targets uses ENSG00000146648[68, 69]. Ensembl increments that suffix when the entity changes, so strip it for joins and keep it for provenance[70].

    Trials join through the NCT number, “NCT” followed by 8 digits[71]. The sponsor’s own protocol number sits in a separate field, and other registry ids go under secondary ids[72]. The ClinicalTrials.gov API version 2 returns one study by NCT number with no API key[73]. For example, NCT02540993 is a Phase 3 trial of finerenone in type 2 diabetes with diabetic kidney disease, the compound in CPD-0102’s row[74, 75].

    From clean table to documented model

    The data and AI lens adds a few duties on top of the cleaning. First, define the context of use: the question the model answers and the decision it informs. The FDA’s January 2025 draft guidance (non-binding) ties the evidence needed to model risk, which combines model influence and decision consequence, and it asks for test data kept apart from development data. It leaves drug discovery uses out of scope[76]. Document the dataset with a datasheet (motivation, composition, collection, recommended uses)[77] and the model with a model card (intended use, evaluation conditions)[78].

    Second, split honestly. Scaffold splits put structurally different molecules in different subsets and give a harder, more realistic test than random splits[79]. Labels are imbalanced in this sector, and on imbalanced data the precision-recall plot is more informative than the ROC plot[80].

    Third, choose retrieval parts by measurement on your own records. No text embedding method wins on every task in the MTEB benchmark[81], and BM25 keyword search remains a strong baseline[82]. A cross-encoder re-ranker scores each query and passage together, and that design topped a public passage-ranking leaderboard when published[83]. Strong LLM judges reached over 80% agreement with humans but show position and verbosity biases, so calibrate them against expert labels first[84]. A tool that suggests MedDRA terms from verbatim text is tested against a gold set coded in one pinned MedDRA version. For AI in drug development, the ten FDA-EMA guiding principles of January 2026 ask for a clear context of use and documented data governance, including traceable provenance and processing steps[85]. They are high-level principles meant to inform future guidance and standards[85]. The 2021 good machine learning practice principles were written for medical devices, not medicines; they also ask teams to monitor deployed models and manage re-training[86].

    Example data

    The DMPK table holds in vitro results that join to potency on compound and batch. Check that every clearance value carries a unit that matches its matrix, that every logD has its pH, and that species is never blank.

    The HTS plate table is the quality record behind a primary screen. Recompute one Z’ from the control columns, then look for a spread stored as a percentage, a missing edge check, a plate accepted against the rule, and a sample-based Z in the Z’ column.

    The CAR-T release table is a cell therapy record. Look at the two VCN denominators, the E:T ratio beside every lysis value, the MFI where a bead-calibrated density belongs, and the missing donor id.

    The AAV titre table is a gene therapy release record. Look for a qPCR titre with no reference standard, a dose unit in a titre column, a titre with no method, and one lot with two reportable values.

    The RNA release table holds results for an mRNA drug substance, two mRNA-LNP drug-product lots made from it, and an antisense oligonucleotide. Look for an integrity result with no method, an encapsulation value on the wrong scale, a drug-product lot with no drug-substance lot, and an oligonucleotide with no modification map.

    The join table links internal compound ids to PubChem, ChEMBL, UniProt and Open Targets. Look at how the mesylate batch and the first-block-only key break grouping, and how a mouse accession hides behind the right protein name.

    The SDTM AE extract is a clinical record in submission shape. Check the subject id format against the other rows, the empty dictionary term, the date format and the serious flag.

    The ICSR table shows safety case intake. Look for the case with no identifiable patient, the reaction in free text, the missing receipt date and the product that cannot be identified.

    The ADC table is a conjugate release and stability record. Look for an average DAR with no method, plasma stability with no species, a retained DAR with no timepoint, and one lot with two averages and no flag for which is reportable.

    Try it

    1. In the messy DMPK table, CPD-0003 shows 12 mL/min/kg in the CL_int column[data]. What went wrong, and what would you need to convert it back?
    Answer

    The good table holds 65 µL/min/mg protein from rat liver microsomes for the same row[data]. The messy value is a scaled whole-body clearance stored in the in vitro column: the matrix still says liver microsomes, but the unit is per kg of body weight[data]. A loader can catch it by checking the unit against the matrix. To convert back you need the scaling factors that were used, such as mg of microsomal protein per g of liver and g of liver per kg of body weight[8]. Without them, keep the 65 µL/min/mg value and put any scaled clearance in its own column[data].

    1. Assume, for this exercise only, that the potency assay’s Km for ATP is 10 µM. What is the Ki of CPD-0003, and what IC50 would a lab running 1 mM ATP report for it[data]?
    Answer

    The table runs ATP at 10 µM, so [S]/Km is 1 and Ki = 3.1 / 2 = 1.55 nM[data]. At 1 mM (1,000 µM), [S]/Km is 100, so the IC50 would be 1.55 × 101 = 157 nM, which is 1.7 pIC50 units weaker for the same molecule (log10 of 157 / 3.1)[1, 3].

    1. From the CAR-T release table, which lots can be ranked on specific lysis, and what is their mean? The messy row for CT-LOT-2024-009 gives VCN as 3.1 copies per µg of DNA; what VCN per CAR-positive cell should the row hold? From the ADC table, what is wrong with the two DAR rows for ADC-LOT-2024-001[data, data]?
    Answer

    CT-LOT-2024-007, -008 and -009 were tested at E:T 5:1, giving 62%, 55% and 71%; their mean is 188 / 3 = 62.7%[data]. CT-LOT-2024-010 ran at 1:1, so its 48% cannot join that ranking, and the messy row with no E:T cannot be used at all[data].

    For CT-LOT-2024-009 the good table has 3.1 copies per cell and 52.0% CAR-positive cells, so VCN per CAR-positive cell is 3.1 / 0.520 = 5.96[data]. That is the form FDA recommends reporting[18]. A per-µg value cannot be turned into either number without more data[data].

    The first messy ADC row gives 3.9 with no method, and a second row gives 4.3 by UV for the same lot: 0.4 apart, about 10% of 3.9[data]. With no method on the first row, nobody can show it is the HIC release value. Keep one reportable DAR from the release method and move the UV value to a characterisation table[data].

    1. Screen the messy ICSR intake table. How many of the four cases are valid ICSRs today, and when is CASE-2024-0102 due under EU rules if it is serious[data]?
    Answer

    Case 0101 has no identifiable patient and case 0104 has no identifiable product, so 2 of 4 fail the four-element test[data]. Case 0103 has all four elements but no receipt date, so its clock cannot start; case 0102 is valid but its reaction is free text and needs MedDRA coding with the version recorded[data].

    So 2 of 4 are valid ICSRs today: CASE-2024-0102 and CASE-2024-0103[data].

    CASE-2024-0102 was received on 2024-05-03 and is serious, so under EU rules it is due within 15 days, by 2024-05-18[data][35].

    1. Using the join table, explain why the two batches of CPD-0101 stop grouping in the messy version. Then, in the messy SDTM AE extract, explain what goes wrong with USUBJID 101-0001 and with the start date 02/04/2024[data, data].
    Answer

    Messy row 2 stores the mesylate key YLMAHDNUQAMNNX-UHFFFAOYSA-N, which differs from the parent key in its first block, so it joins to CID 123596 and CHEMBL1642 instead of CID 5291 and CHEMBL941[data]. Follow ChEMBL’s parent link or strip the salt before the join[62].

    USUBJID 101-0001 drops the study prefix that the other rows carry (STUDY-001-101-0001), so this subject’s headache will not join to demographics or exposure[data]. The FDA expects one USUBJID per subject, identical across all datasets[32]. The date 02/04/2024 is day/month/year for 2 April 2024, but a loader that assumes month first reads 4 February: 58 days early[data]. SDTM dates use ISO 8601, so it should read 2024-04-02, with the time if it was collected[32, 33].

    1. In the messy HTS plate table, which plate is accepted against the rule, and what Z’ would a loader compute for P-0001 if it read 22.5 as the min-control standard deviation[data]?
    Answer

    P-0004 has Z’ 0.28 and an edge or drift effect of 27%, so it fails both parts of the example rule (Z’ at least 0.5, edge or drift under 20%) and should be rejected and re-run[data]. P-0003 cannot be judged on edge effects, because the check was not recorded[data].

    For P-0001 the correct Z’ is 1 minus 3 × (2,100 + 900) / (52,000 minus 4,000) = 1 minus 9,000 / 48,000 = 0.81[data][10]. The 22.5 is a coefficient of variation (900 / 4,000 = 22.5%). Read as an SD in signal units it gives 1 minus 3 × (2,100 + 22.5) / 48,000 = 0.87, a better-looking number from a wrong input[data].

    1. In the messy AAV titre table, AAV-LOT-2025-001 has two titres marked reportable: 2.1E+13 vg/mL by ddPCR and 2.6E+13 vg/mL by qPCR[data]. How far apart are they, and what happens to a dose if the wrong one is used?
    Answer

    The qPCR titre is 2.6 / 2.1 = 1.24 times the ddPCR titre, about 24% higher[data]. The good table names ddPCR as the reportable method[data]. If the dose volume is worked out from the qPCR titre and the ddPCR value is right, the patient gets 2.1 / 2.6 = 81% of the intended vector genomes[data]. ddPCR needs no standard curve, while a qPCR titre depends on its reference standard: messy row 2 leaves it blank, and messy row 5 repeats the qPCR titre and marks it reportable[21][data].

    1. In the messy RNA release table, a dashboard shows the mean RNA encapsulation of lots LNP-DP-2025-031 and LNP-DP-2025-032. What does it show, and what should it show? Then name the two other rows that stop you from tracing a result back to what was made[data].
    Answer

    Messy: (0.94 + 92) / 2 = 46.47, so about 46.5%. Good: (94 + 92) / 2 = 93.0%[data]. One value stored as a fraction in a percent column halved the mean, and a release check would read lot 031 as a failure.

    Row 4: LNP-DP-2025-032 has no mRNA drug-substance lot, so its results cannot be traced to MRNA-DS-2025-011 or to the process that made it[data]. The EMA assessment of an mRNA vaccine shows why the link matters: RNA integrity fell in the first batches of a new drug-substance process[24]. Row 5: ASO-DS-2025-004 has a sequence id but no modification map, so nothing says which chemistry the 90.5% purity belongs to; an oligonucleotide’s identity is its sequence plus its sugar and backbone chemistry[23]. Row 1 is also unusable for comparison: an integrity value with no method cannot be set beside the drug-product result measured by capillary gel electrophoresis[data].
