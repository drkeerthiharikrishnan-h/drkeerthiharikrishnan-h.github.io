Biopharma for Data and AI engineers

Map · Machine · Frontier: the data objects, standards and model rules behind a medicine

Approved drugs are rare, so pipeline datasets have few positive examples

  • Of 100 drugs entering Phase I, about 8 are approved
  • Phase II is the steepest drop: 28.9% advance
  • Lack of efficacy explains 40-50% of trial failures

Source: BIO, Informa Pharma Intelligence and QLS Advisors, 2021; Sun, Gao, Hu and Zhou, 2022

Speaker notes

Each stage makes different data at a different volume: discovery screens 200,000 to 1,000,000+ compounds per project. The clinical figures come from 9,704 programmes tracked from 2011 to 2020. Ask the room: in a dataset of past programmes, how many positive labels would you have?

Every dataset ends up in a CTD module, so trace each pipeline to its reader

  • Module 3: quality (CMC); Module 4: nonclinical
  • Module 5: clinical study reports, datasets and reviewer's guides
  • Sent as eCTD, version 3.2.2 or 4.0 at the FDA

Source: US FDA, 2017; US FDA, 2026; US FDA (CDER, CBER), 2026

Speaker notes

CMC means chemistry, manufacturing and controls. eCTD is required for NDAs, BLAs and commercial INDs. Module 1 is regional; Modules 2 to 5 are common to all regions. Ask which module a pipeline feeds: that tells you whether clinical reviewers, quality reviewers or pharmacologists read it.

Each stage has its own system of record, so join on its identifiers

  • Lab: LIMS, ELN; trials: EDC, eCOA, IRT, CTMS, eTMF
  • Plant: MES at ISA-95 Level 3, ERP at Level 4
  • Regulator hand-offs: SEND, SDTM/ADaM, E2B(R3)

Source: Raviteja and Gupta, JSS College of Pharmacy, 2013; European Medicines Agency (GCP IWG), 2023; ISA; CDISC, 2021; CDISC, 2024; CDISC, 2021; European Medicines Agency, 2020

Speaker notes

Regulatory product data follow IDMP, five ISO standards that EMA runs through SPOR. The FDA asks sponsors to get a UNII early, so study data links to one substance. Real-world data comes from health records, claims, registries and digital health devices. Ask: which system holds the golden record for each entity?

Trial data follows one path, and reviewers check every number traces back

  • CDASH collects; SDTM organises, required by FDA and PMDA
  • ADaM analyses and traces back to SDTM
  • One subject keeps one USUBJID across every dataset

Source: CDISC, 2023; CDISC, 2024; CDISC, 2021; US FDA (CDER, CBER), 2026

Speaker notes

The FDA calls wrong USUBJID use a common error that often forces a resubmission. Traceability lets a reviewer relate counts in report tables to the data; the FDA lists it among the most problematic issues in data conversion. Define-XML describes every dataset, variable and controlled term. For a data engineer this is column-level lineage, kept as evidence.

Safety cases need four elements and versioned codes, so check both

  • Four elements: reporter, patient, suspect reaction, suspect medicine
  • EU: serious cases in 15 days; non-serious in 90
  • Coded in MedDRA and WHODrug; one version per study

Source: European Medicines Agency and Heads of Medicines Agencies, 2017; MedDRA MSSO (ICH), 2026; Uppsala Monitoring Centre; US FDA (CDER, CBER), 2026

Speaker notes

Without any one of the four elements, a case does not qualify as an ICSR, an individual case safety report. GVP Module VI asks that everyone who gave case information is recorded, so duplicates can be detected. Pooled safety data should use one current WHODrug version, not a mix. Store the dictionary version with every coded term.

Each question has its own vocabulary, so use stable codes as join keys

  • Adverse events: MedDRA; lab tests: LOINC; substances: UNII
  • Health records: FHIR resources coded in SNOMED CT, RxNorm
  • Real-world data: OMOP CDM across DARWIN EU's 40 partners

Source: US FDA (CDER, CBER), 2026; MedDRA MSSO (ICH), 2026; US National Library of Medicine, 2015; HL7 International, 2023; SNOMED International; US National Library of Medicine, 2025; OHDSI; European Medicines Agency

Speaker notes

DARWIN EU covers about 250 million patients, with each partner converted to one data model. FHIR's research resources are at low maturity, so build on stable ones such as Patient and Observation. Discovery uses open vocabularies: UniProt for proteins, ChEBI for chemicals, Gene Ontology for function. Use stable identifiers as join keys, not free-text names.

In a validated pipeline, retraining is a change that needs control

  • Validation extent follows a documented risk assessment
  • New model, dictionary or mapping version: change control
  • ALCOA++: attributable, contemporaneous, original, traceable data

Source: European Commission, 2011; ISPE, 2022; European Medicines Agency (GCP IWG), 2023

Speaker notes

Annex 11 clauses 1, 10 and 11 cover risk, change control and periodic review. In ML, a validation set tunes hyperparameters; the FDA draft calls that "tuning data". In GxP, validation is documented proof that a system fits its intended use. Say which meaning you intend in every meeting.

Regulators judge AI by its context of use and risk

  • FDA draft (January 2025): model risk combines influence and consequence
  • EMA: freeze before unblinding (pivotal trials); use hold-out sets once
  • Merck & Co. and Novo Nordisk draft study reports

Source: US FDA, 2025; European Medicines Agency, 2024; Merck & Co., 2025; Anthropic

Speaker notes

Merck's platform is designed to operate with oversight by qualified medical writers. Bayer, as a signal rather than production evidence, studied one French drug-safety team of 3 for about four months, drafting case documentation letters; more than 70% of FDA staff used Elsa voluntarily, per FDA internal data. Novo Nordisk and Sanofi figures are vendor-published. Ask of any benchmark: which baseline, and was the test data independent of training?

Biopharma on one page, for Data and AI engineers

The chain, ten terms, five questions, cases.

The chain in one line

Trial data: collect in CDASH → organise in SDTM → analyse in ADaM → report in a clinical study report (CSR, ICH E3) → submit in CTD Module 5, as eCTD[1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6]. Safety cases travel as E2B(R3) ICSRs; animal study data as SEND[7, 8].

Ten terms

  • SDTM: CDISC’s shape for trial data, in classes such as Events and Findings; required by the FDA and Japan’s PMDA[2].
  • ADaM: analysis datasets (ADSL, BDS, OCCDS), traceable back to SDTM[3].
  • Define-XML: the metadata file describing every submitted dataset; FDA and PMDA require it[9].
  • USUBJID: one ID per subject across every study and dataset in an application[10].
  • ICSR: one suspected side-effect case. Needs four elements: reporter, patient, suspect reaction, suspect medicine; without one it is incomplete[11].
  • MedDRA: adverse-event terminology in five levels, 27 System Organ Classes at the top[12].
  • IDMP / SPOR: ISO standards for substances and products; EMA’s master data for them[13].
  • FHIR: health-record exchange as resources such as Patient and Observation[14, 15].
  • OMOP CDM: common model for observational data; DARWIN EU’s partners convert to it[16, 17].
  • Context of use: the role and scope of an AI model for one question, in the FDA’s draft guidance[18].

Five questions to ask

  1. Which standard and version: SDTM, MedDRA, WHODrug, FHIR release?[10]
  2. Where is lineage from source record to final output recorded?[10]
  3. Is it GxP data (covered by good-practice rules), and which rule applies: US Part 11 or EU Annex 11? Test it against ALCOA++, EMA’s list of data-quality attributes[19, 20, 21].
  4. What is the model’s context of use, and how high are its influence and decision consequence?[18]
  5. Is the test data independent of training, and who reviews the output?[22]

Cases and one signal

  • Merck & Co. drafts clinical study reports on an internal generative AI platform. It is designed to operate with oversight by qualified medical writers[23].
  • Novo Nordisk’s NovoScribe drafts reports and patient materials from domain-expert-approved text; figures are vendor-published[24, 25].
  • Signal, not yet shown in production: Bayer. Bayer studied one French drug-safety team of 3 over about four months[26]. Drafting case letters with an internal AI tool saved 23.3% of time on average[26]. All users kept using it[26].
