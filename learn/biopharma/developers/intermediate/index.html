    Three identifier rules break joins at this level

    • Ensembl ids carry a version after a dot
    • E2B(R3): sender id C.1.1, case id C.1.8.1
    • Record which system minted each id

    Source: Ruffier et al., Ensembl (Database, Oxford), 2017; US FDA; European Medicines Agency (GCP IWG), 2023

    Speaker notes

    The beginner module gave the formats; this level adds the rules that break joins. UniProt's EGFR cross-reference gives ENSG00000146648.22, with the suffix. In FDA submissions, follow-up safety reports reuse the C.1.1 id of the initial report. Ask the room: where in your pipeline is each format validated today?

    Potency and DMPK values compare only under their conditions

    • Ki = IC50 / (1 + [S]/Km) for a competitive inhibitor
    • Microsome and hepatocyte clearance use different units
    • A logD without its pH cannot be compared

    Source: Kalliokoski, Kramer, Vulpetti and Gedeck (PLOS ONE), 2013; Acta Pharmaceutica, 2019; Journal of Cheminformatics, 2023

    Speaker notes

    The mean of pIC50 is the geometric mean of IC50; 1 nM is pIC50 9. At an assumed Km of 10 uM, moving from 10 uM to 1 mM ATP makes the same compound look about 50 times weaker. Ask: which of your tables store ATP, species and pH as columns?

    A censored value needs its relation stored apart from its number

    • ChEMBL activity 909964: imatinib vs EGFR, IC50 greater than 100 uM
    • Stored as relation ">" plus 100000 nM; no pChEMBL
    • Published 0.04 uM kept beside standard 40 nM

    Source: EMBL-EBI ChEMBL; EMBL-EBI ChEMBL; EMBL-EBI (ChEMBL)

    Speaker notes

    For common activity types such as IC50, ChEMBL's downloads keep the original value and units and add standardised columns beside them. That is the pattern for any internal loader. Ask: what happens to a greater-than value in your averaging code?

    A screening plate counts only if its own controls separate

    • Compute Z' from each plate's control wells
    • Z' from 0.5 to 1 marks an excellent assay
    • Check every plate for edge and drift effects

    Source: Varbanov, Kuttler, Banfi, Turcatti and Dyson (PLOS ONE), 2017; Zhang, Wang, Sun and Zhang (Bioinformatics), 2020; Eli Lilly & Company and NCATS (Assay Guidance Manual)

    Speaker notes

    Z' is 1 minus 3 times the sum of the control SDs over the difference of the control means. Edge or drift effects under 20% are generally considered insignificant. A Z from sample wells is a different number and needs its own column. Ask: does your plate loader recompute Z' or trust the instrument file?

    Antibody and conjugate values carry their method and orientation

    • KD equals off-rate over on-rate: check it on import
    • Record antigen form and orientation: avidity tightens apparent binding
    • One reportable DAR per lot, from a named method

    Source: mAbs (Taylor & Francis), 2023; Scientific Reports, 2014; mAbs (Taylor & Francis), 2020

    Speaker notes

    One study found native SEC-MS and HIC statistically equivalent for average DAR, so the usual defect is an unrecorded method. Plasma stability needs species and timepoint. Each conjugate lot traces to its antibody lot and drug-linker lot [D:adc-dar]. Ask: which of these would your schema reject today?

    Cell and gene therapy results need the right denominator

    • Report VCN per CAR-positive cell
    • Lysis needs E:T; target antigen density is not MFI
    • AAV titre is vg/mL; patient dose is vg/kg

    Source: US FDA (CBER), 2024; Poncelet, Lecompte, Bauters and Mullier (MDPI IJMS), 2026; CADTH (NCBI Bookshelf); World Health Organization, 2022

    Speaker notes

    FDA recommends VCN per CAR-positive cell, derived from the per-cell value and the CAR-positive percentage. CT-LOT-2024-008: 2.4 / 0.387 = 6.20 copies per CAR-positive cell [D:cart-release]. MFI is in arbitrary units and needs standardisation between instruments; calibration beads convert it to antibodies bound per cell. In one study qPCR titres averaged 64 times ddPCR; correcting the qPCR standard cut the gap to 5.5 times. For mRNA vaccines, WHO guidance treats mRNA integrity as a critical quality attribute for release, and encapsulation is controlled lot by lot. Ask: which denominator does your VCN column use today, and where is it written down?

    Clinical and nonclinical data share one tabulation model

    • SDTM is required for FDA and PMDA submissions
    • SEND applies SDTM to nonclinical studies
    • Define-XML is required for every study submitted

    Source: CDISC, 2024; CDISC, 2021; CDISC

    Speaker notes

    The FDA names improper USUBJID as a common error that often forces resubmission. SDTM, the model behind SEND, keeps the result as collected apart from the result in standard units. For vaccine lot consistency, FDA's influenza guidance wants each GMT-ratio interval inside 0.67 to 1.5. Ask: where would a mismatched USUBJID first show up in your stack?

    A coded safety term is meaningless without its MedDRA version

    • MedDRA releases twice a year: March X.0, September X.1
    • Valid ICSR: reporter, patient, reaction, product
    • Follow-ups reuse the initial report's C.1.1 id

    Source: MedDRA MSSO, 2009; European Medicines Agency and Heads of Medicines Agencies, 2017; US FDA

    Speaker notes

    Under EU rules, serious cases are due within 15 days of receipt and non-serious within 90. E2B(R3) has been mandatory for EudraVigilance since 30 June 2022. Ask: does your case table store the dictionary version per coded term?

    Manufacturing and real-world data have their own keys

    • Stability keys add condition and timepoint
    • OMOP: standard concept ids; 0 means unmapped
    • Local custom concepts sit above two billion

    Source: ICH; OHDSI community; OHDSI; OHDSI CDM Working Group

    Speaker notes

    A failing first result and its retest stay as two linked rows. Custom concepts are never standard and go only in source concept fields. DARWIN EU reports about 40 data partners as of 2026; its partners convert their data to OMOP. Ask: how many of your mapped rows have concept id 0?

    Join compounds on the full InChIKey of the parent structure

    • Imatinib key returns CHEMBL941; the salt returns CHEMBL1642
    • First block SUBDBMMJDZJVOS matched 60 PubChem compounds
    • Salt vs base weight: 589.7 vs 493.6, about 19%

    Source: EMBL-EBI ChEMBL; EMBL-EBI ChEMBL; NCBI PubChem; NCBI PubChem; NCBI PubChem

    Speaker notes

    Omeprazole (CHEMBL1503) and esomeprazole (CHEMBL1201320) share the first block and are separate ChEMBL parents. Keep salt and form on the batch, not the compound. Ask: which key does your registry export?

    Versioned or reformatted ids fail silently against public APIs

    • UniProt P00533 links to ENSG00000146648.22

    Learn

    Identifiers: three rules that break joins

    The beginner module gave the formats. This level adds three rules that break joins when they are missed. The public example values below were retrieved on 3 Nov 2026[1, 2, 3].

    First, an Ensembl gene id can carry a version number after a dot. The version goes up when the underlying entity changes[4]. UniProt’s cross-reference for EGFR gives ENSG00000146648.22, with the suffix[2]. Store the bare id and the version in separate columns, because some public APIs key on the bare id (see the joins below).

    Second, a safety case carries two E2B(R3) identifiers: the sender’s report id (C.1.1) and the worldwide unique case id (C.1.8.1). For FDA submissions, follow-up reports reuse the C.1.1 id given to the initial report[5]. A case table needs both columns.

    Third, record which system minted each id. A compound id from the registry and a ChEMBL id from a public release are different facts, even when they describe the same molecule[6, 7]. Store identifiers as text and validate the format where data enters, never deep inside a pipeline. Internal ids in this module look like CPD-0001, CT-LOT-2024-007 or STUDY-001.

    Discovery: screens, small molecules and probes

    Screening plates come before any IC50. Each plate has positive and negative control wells. Z’ uses their means and standard deviations: 1 minus 3 times the sum of the two SDs, divided by the difference of the two means[8]. Values between 0.5 and 1 mark an excellent assay[9]. Compute Z’ for each plate from that plate’s own controls, and check each plate for edge and drift effects. Effects under 20% are generally considered insignificant[10]. A Z’ uses the two controls, while a Z can use sample wells as its negative reference. They are different numbers, so they need separate columns[11].

    Potency tables look simple and hide the most errors. IC50 is the concentration that inhibits a reaction by half under one set of conditions. Ki is an inhibition constant that does not depend on substrate concentration. For a competitive inhibitor the Cheng-Prusoff relation links them: Ki = IC50 / (1 + [S]/Km)[12]. So a kinase IC50 measured at high ATP and one measured near Km for ATP are different numbers for the same compound. The ATP concentration is part of the value, which is why the potency table carries it as a column[12, 13].

    Potency is often reported as pIC50, the negative log10 of the molar IC50, so 1 nM is pIC50 9[14]. Averaging pIC50 values gives the geometric mean of the IC50 values. For replicates of 10 nM and 1000 nM, the arithmetic mean is 505 nM, while the mean pIC50 of 7 corresponds to 100 nM[14]. Pick one rule, write it down and apply it in code.

    Censored values need their own column. ChEMBL activity 909964 records imatinib against EGFR as an IC50 greater than 100 uM. ChEMBL stores the relation greater than (>) apart from the number 100000 nM[15] and computes no pChEMBL value for it[16]. A pipeline that drops the relation turns “inactive at the top dose” into “IC50 of 100000 nM”. It then averages that number with real measurements.

    ChEMBL also shows how to normalise units without losing the source. Activity 388530 was published as 0.04 uM. ChEMBL keeps that value and unit, and adds a standard value of 40 nM and a pChEMBL of 7.40[17, 18]. Copy that pattern: original columns stay as received, standardised columns sit beside them, and the conversion is code you can test.

    DMPK (drug metabolism and pharmacokinetics) results add their own conditions. Intrinsic clearance from liver microsomes is reported per mg of protein. From hepatocytes it is reported per million cells. Neither is the scaled in vivo clearance in mL/min/kg[19]. Species matters too, because metabolic stability differs between preclinical species and human[19]. LogD (how a compound splits between octanol and water) changes with pH, so a logD without its pH cannot be compared[20]. The join key across potency and DMPK is compound_id plus batch_id, because salt and solid form belong to the batch.

    Chemical probes (tool compounds used to test a target’s biology) have published criteria. For epigenetic targets, the minimum is potency under 100 nM, over 30-fold selectivity within the target family, on-target cell activity under 1 uM, and an inactive close analog as a negative control; other targets need criteria suited to them[21]. Because a selectivity fold is a ratio of two potencies, a single-concentration percent inhibition cannot stand in for it. Pooled CRISPR screens report a log2 fold change and a false discovery rate per gene, each relative to a named control sample[22]. Genes must be mapped to approved HGNC symbols before guides are grouped[23].

    Biologics, cell and gene therapies

    For antibodies (protein drugs that bind one target), binding is often measured by surface plasmon resonance (SPR). The dissociation constant KD is the ratio of the off-rate to the on-rate[24]. That gives a free consistency check on import: recompute KD from the two rates and flag rows that disagree. Assay orientation matters too. A two-armed antibody binding an immobilised antigen can look tighter through avidity, so the antigen form and orientation belong in the record[25].

    Antibody-drug conjugates (antibodies that carry a cytotoxic payload) report an average drug-to-antibody ratio (DAR) from a named method, such as HIC or native mass spectrometry. In one study, native SEC-MS and HIC gave statistically equivalent average DAR values across several conjugates[26]. So the usual defect is not a large gap between methods. It is a DAR with no method recorded, or two values for one lot with no flag for the reportable one[data]. Plasma stability is measured separately in each species’ plasma over days, so species and timepoint are required fields[27]. A conjugate lot links to the antibody lot and the drug-linker lot it was made from. That genealogy is the join path from a release result to an in vivo study that dosed the lot.

    CAR-T products (patient T cells engineered to carry a chimeric antigen receptor) are released lot by lot against a CMC panel that includes potency[28]. FDA recommends reporting vector copy number (VCN) per CAR-positive cell. You can derive it from the per-cell value and the CAR-positive percentage[28]. That division holds only when both numbers describe the same cell population, so record the population each was measured on. A value per microgram of DNA has a third denominator and needs its own column. Percent specific lysis means nothing without its effector-to-target (E:T) ratio, so store the ratio with the result. Antigen density on the target cells is an assay condition, not a property of the lot. Fluorescence intensity (MFI) is in arbitrary units and needs standardisation between instruments; convert it to antibodies bound per cell with calibration beads before comparing runs, and keep the reagents the same[29, 30]. In an autologous product each lot is made from one patient’s cells, so a missing donor id breaks the chain of identity.

    Gene therapies built on AAV vectors (engineered viruses that deliver a gene) use two different units. The vector lot has a genome titre, a concentration in vector genomes per mL. The patient dose is vector genomes per kg of body weight (vg/kg), as for onasemnogene abeparvovec, given once as an intravenous infusion[31, 32]. Titre is measured by qPCR against a standard curve, or by ddPCR, which counts copies without one[33, 34]. In one study, qPCR titres for five samples averaged 64 times the ddPCR titres. The qPCR standard held 11.5 times fewer copies than its label stated; with the re-measured value, the gap fell to 5.5 times[34]. So method, amplified region and reference standard belong with every titre, and one release method gives the reportable value[data].

    mRNA vaccines (RNA medicines that carry instructions for a protein) add their own release columns. WHO guidance treats the integrity of the mRNA as a critical quality attribute for release. Integrity is the proportion of the mRNA that is the right size and carries its 5′ cap and poly(A) tail; capping efficiency, fragments and double-stranded RNA are controlled too[35]. The mRNA is packed into lipid nanoparticles (LNPs). Their controls typically include the identity, quantity and purity of the lipids, particle size and its spread (polydispersity), and the share of RNA encapsulated[35]. mRNA can degrade during encapsulation, so an integrity value from the drug substance does not describe the filled lot[35]. One electrophoresis method can report both integrity and percentage encapsulated, so each row names the attribute and the method[35]. A drug product lot links to its mRNA lot and its lipid lots, the same kind of genealogy as an antibody-drug conjugate[data].

    Recombinant protein batches report titer, purity and endotoxin. Titer in g/L and mg/L differ by a factor of 1000[36]. Endotoxin limits are set in endotoxin units (EU), from the maximum dose that can be given within one hour[37], so a result reported as a mass concentration cannot be checked against them. The join key across release testing and in vivo studies is the lot id, carried unchanged from the batch record to the study that used it.

    Nonclinical, clinical and safety: from case report form to submission

    Clinical data follows a fixed chain. CDASH standardises how sites collect data, and is traceable into SDTM[38]. SDTM organises the collected data into domains and is required for submissions to the FDA and PMDA[39]. ADaM holds the analysis datasets, traceable back to SDTM[40]. Define-XML describes the structure of each dataset and is required for every study in an electronic submission[41]. Each dataset ships as a SAS XPORT version 5 file, one dataset per file, such as ae.xpt[42].

    Nonclinical studies use SEND, the SDTM implementation for animal studies[43]. The SDTM model behind SEND keeps the result as collected (—ORRES and —ORRESU) apart from the result in standard units (—STRESN and —STRESU). The two hyphens stand for the two-character domain code, so the body weight (BW) domain gives BWORRES and BWORRESU, and BWSTRESN and BWSTRESU[44, 45]. Test codes come from controlled terminology; body weight is BW[46]. USUBJID must be unique across all studies in the submission here too[42].

    For an engineer, the FDA’s technical guide names the main failure points. An improper USUBJID is a common error that often forces a resubmission. Traceability from analysis results back to source data is one of the most problematic issues in conversion[42]. In the adverse event (AE) domain, AETERM is the reported text and AEDECOD the dictionary term. AESTDTC is an ISO 8601 date, and AESER takes the values Y or N[47]. USUBJID is the join across every domain: AE, demographics, exposure, labs.

    Vaccine programmes may add a clinical lot-consistency study. Three consecutively made lots are compared by the ratio of geometric mean titres (GMT) for each strain[48]. In FDA’s influenza vaccine guidance, the two-sided 95% confidence interval of each ratio should lie entirely within 0.67 and 1.5[48]. A point estimate inside the margin is not enough. Each ratio also needs a named numerator and denominator and a timepoint[data]. WHO describes the comparison as pair-wise and post-vaccination[49].

    Adverse events are coded in MedDRA, a five-level hierarchy from system organ class down to lowest level term, with 27 system organ classes[50]. MedDRA releases twice a year. The March X.0 release can change every level; the September X.1 release changes only preferred and lowest level terms[51]. Users are asked to assess how each version change affects their outputs[52]. So the coded term needs a dictionary version beside it. Counts produced under two versions are not one series.

    Pharmacovigilance (PV, the monitoring of side effects) works on individual case safety reports (ICSRs). A valid ICSR needs four elements: an identifiable reporter, a single identifiable patient, a suspect adverse reaction and a suspect medicinal product[53]. Under EU rules, serious cases are due within 15 days of receipt and non-serious ones within 90 days[53]. The ISO ICSR format based on ICH E2B(R3) has been mandatory for EudraVigilance since 30 June 2022[54]. The join across intake, follow-ups and coding is therefore the case id plus the MedDRA version.

    Manufacturing, real-world data and regulatory records

    In CMC (chemistry, manufacturing and controls), a specification lists tests, references to analytical procedures and acceptance criteria[55, 56]. A result outside its criterion is out of specification (OOS) and starts a documented investigation. A passing retest does not erase the original failing result unless a laboratory error is documented[57]. The beginner module’s batch table shows the pattern: run 1 at 93.8% and the retest at 101.0% sit in two rows under one investigation id[data].

    Stability studies add storage condition and timepoint to the key. In the ICH Q1A(R2) general case, long-term studies run at 25 C and 60% relative humidity (or 30 C and 65%), with at least 12 months of data at submission. Accelerated studies run at 40 C and 75%, with at least 6 months of data at submission[58]. ICH is revising its stability guideline series under the new title ICH Q1 Stability Testing of Drug Substances and Drug Products; its February 2026 work plan expected Step 4 adoption in November 2026[59]. A stability record joins to release data on lot, condition, timepoint and method version.

    Real-world data (from health records, claims and registries) is often converted to the OMOP common data model[60, 61]. Each source code maps to a standard concept, and only standard concepts go in fields ending in _concept_id[62]. The original code stays in the source value field, and a concept id of 0 means the code was not mapped[63]. Ids up to 2,000,000,000 are reserved for the standard vocabularies. A local code can get a custom concept above that range; it is never standard and goes only in a _source_concept_id field[64, 65]. DARWIN EU, the EMA network for real-world evidence, reports about 40 data partners as of 2026. Its partners convert their data to OMOP[66].

    Regulatory records sit in the Common Technical Document: five modules, with Module 1 regional and Modules 2 to 5 common to all regions[67]. eCTD is required for NDAs, BLAs and commercial INDs at the FDA[68]. Product master data follows the five ISO IDMP standards that EMA runs through its SPOR services[69]. For a substance, the FDA asks for a UNII in the trial summary dataset[42].

    Cleaning as a discipline, written as code

    Tidy data has one variable per column, one observation per row and one table per type of observational unit[70]. In practice that means a unit column next to every value, a relation column next to every censored value and a replicate id on every replicate. Conditions such as ATP, pH, E:T, species and timepoint get their own columns, never comments.

    The regulated rule behind “never overwrite” is explicit. Part 11 asks for secure, computer-generated, time-stamped audit trails. Record changes must not obscure what was recorded before[71]. EU GMP Annex 11 asks that a system audit trail be considered on the basis of a risk assessment, and that changes be made only under a defined, controlled procedure[72]. The EMA’s guideline for clinical trial systems lists ten ALCOA++ attributes: attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original, accurate, complete, consistent, enduring, available and traceable[7]. In a pipeline this becomes an append-only raw layer and standardised columns built beside the raw ones. Every derived value names the code version and the dictionary or mapping version that produced it.

    De-duplication needs a key, not a guess. Two rows for the same compound, batch and assay are either replicates (give them a replicate id) or a duplicate load (find and remove the second load, with a record of why). Provenance columns on every row make that call possible: source system, source record id, load time, file checksum, mapping version[7, 73].

    Tests are the evidence that the rules hold. A potency loader can assert that pIC50 matches the IC50 column within rounding[14]. A binding loader can assert that KD matches the rate ratio[24]. A plate loader can recompute Z’ from the control columns[8]. A batch loader can recompute the OOS flag from result and limit[57]. A subject loader can assert one USUBJID format across domains[42]. Store the test run with the data release, and the validation record writes itself.

    Joining internal records to public sources

    Start with compounds. Send the full InChIKey of the parent structure to ChEMBL’s molecule endpoint. Imatinib’s key returns CHEMBL941[74]. The key of imatinib mesylate, the salt, returns a different record, CHEMBL1642. ChEMBL lists CHEMBL941 as its parent[75]. PubChem behaves the same way: CID 5291 for the free base and CID 123596 for the salt, with molecular weights of 493.6 and 589.7[76, 77]. Resolve to the parent, and keep the salt on the batch. Otherwise one compound splits into two public ids, and a conversion from ug/mL to uM with the wrong weight is off by about 19%.

    Never join on the first block alone. Esomeprazole (the S form) and omeprazole (the racemate) share the first block SUBDBMMJDZJVOS and differ in the second[78, 79]. A PubChem search on that block alone returned 60 compounds[80]. ChEMBL keeps them as separate parents: omeprazole CHEMBL1503 and esomeprazole CHEMBL1201320[81]. A first-block hit is a prompt for review, not a match.

    Targets come next. ChEMBL target CHEMBL203 is human EGFR, a single protein whose component is UniProt P00533[82]. That accession joins ChEMBL activities to UniProt. The UniProt entry links to HGNC:3236 and to Ensembl gene ENSG00000146648.22, with a version suffix[2]. Open Targets keys targets by Ensembl gene id[83]. Sent without the suffix, the id returned EGFR. Sent with it, the query returned no target and no error[84]. Strip the version, store it separately, and treat an empty answer as a failure to investigate.

    Trials join through the registry. The ClinicalTrials.gov v2 API returns one study at /api/v2/studies/ followed by the NCT number, with no API key[85]. Each record carries the sponsor’s own protocol id in orgStudyIdInfo, and lists obsolete or duplicate NCT numbers under nctIdAliases[86]. NCT00171912, for example, records the org study id CSTI571BAU12[3]. That field is the bridge from an internal protocol number to the public record. A request with the zeros dropped, NCT171912, still came back as NCT00171912[3]. The API forgave the error, but a local join on the stored string would not.

    Log every lookup: the value sent, the endpoint, the id returned, the match rule, the source release and the date. ChEMBL reported release ChEMBL_37 of 1 May 2026[1]. Open Targets reported data version 26.09[84]. In a user interface, show the public id and its locator next to any public number, so a scientist can open the record. Ask Open Targets in bulk through its downloads, not through thousands of single queries[87].

    Example data

    This module has more tables than one sitting can hold. Work through the identifier, clinical and manufacturing tables, then pick the two modality tables your project touches. The rest are optional practice.

    The cross-reference log is the developer’s view of the joins above. Compare each messy row with its good twin. Row 1 has no source release or date. Row 2 sent the salt key and got the salt record. Row 3 matched on the first block only and lost the stereocentre. Row 4 sent a versioned Ensembl id and got an empty answer with no error. Row 5 stores an NCT number with its zeros dropped[data].

    The plate table keeps control means and SDs for each plate, so Z’ can be recomputed. In the messy version, look for a CV typed into an SD column, a plate with no edge check, a failing plate marked accepted, and a sample-based Z stored as the control Z’[data].

    The DMPK table carries species, matrix and a unit beside each clearance, and the pH beside each logD. In the messy version, look for a clearance with no unit, a logD with no pH, a scaled in vivo value in the in vitro column, and a row with no species[data].

    The CRISPR screen table gives each guide a gene symbol, a timepoint, a control sample and an FDR. In the messy version, look for a gene alias that will not group with its twin guide, a fold change with no control sample, a p-value in the FDR column, a missing timepoint and a blank gene on a control guide[data].

    The probe dossier keeps a probe, its inactive negative control and an orthogonal probe on one target, with the off-target panel and its version. In the messy version, look for an unlabelled control, a percent inhibition in the selectivity column and a hit relabelled as a probe[data].

    The binding table keeps the two rate constants beside KD, plus antigen form and orientation. In the messy version, look for a pM value in an nM table, a missing antigen form after the orientation changed, a unit typed into the value cell, and a KD that disagrees with its rates[data].

    The conjugate table traces each lot to its antibody lot and drug-linker lot. Compare dar_avg only within one dar_method[data][26]. In the messy version, look for a DAR with no method, stability rows with no species or timepoint, and one lot with two averages[data].

    The CAR-T release table keeps two VCN columns: per total cell and per CAR-positive cell. Percent lysis needs its E:T ratio. Target-cell antigen density is an assay condition in molecules per cell, not an MFI. Note also the lot with no donor id[data].

    The vector titre table gives each titre a method, amplified region, reference standard and unit, and one reportable value per lot. In the messy version, look for a qPCR titre with no standard, a dose unit on a titre, a titre with no method, and a second reportable value for one lot[data].

    The mRNA-LNP table gives each drug product lot its mRNA lot, lipid lot, integrity, encapsulation and particle size, each with its method. In the messy version, look for a size written where integrity belongs, an encapsulation with no method, a missing lipid lot and a drug substance value copied into a drug product row[data].

    The case intake table checks the four minimum elements of an ICSR plus the receipt date and seriousness. In the messy version, look for a case with no identifiable patient, a reaction in free text, a missing receipt date and a product nobody can identify[data][53].

    The lot-consistency table names the numerator, assay, timepoint and margin for every GMT ratio. In the messy version, look for a ratio with no timepoint, a margin widened for one pair, a comparison with no numerator, and a pair marked consistent when its interval crosses the margin[data].

    The SEND body weight slice keeps the result as collected beside the standardised result. In the messy version, look for a standardised unit that differs from the rest, a test code not in the codelist, an animal id that is not unique, and text in a numeric column[data].

    Try it

    1. Which sm-dmpk rows can be ranked against CPD-0001’s intrinsic clearance of 18 µL/min/mg protein? Check the good table first, then the messy one[data].
    Answer

    Only CPD-0005 (31 µL/min/mg protein, human liver microsomes) can be ranked against CPD-0001[data]. CPD-0002 is measured in hepatocytes, per 10^6 cells. CPD-0003 is rat microsomes, and species changes metabolic stability[19].

    In the messy table, row 3 shows 12 mL/min/kg for CPD-0003. That is a scaled in vivo value in the in vitro column, and the loader rejects it. Row 1 has no unit, so even CPD-0001’s own value cannot be used until the unit is restored. Row 4 has no species, so CPD-0005 drops out until the species is filled in[data].

    1. The kinase assay runs at 10 uM ATP. Assume, for this exercise only, that Km for ATP is also 10 uM and the inhibitor is competitive. Estimate Ki for CPD-0003, then predict its IC50 in a 1 mM ATP assay[data].
    Answer

    CPD-0003 has IC50 3.1 nM at 10 uM ATP[data]. Cheng-Prusoff gives Ki = 3.1 / (1 + 10/10) = 1.55 nM[12, 13]. At 1000 uM ATP: IC50 = 1.55 x (1 + 1000/10) = 1.55 x 101 = 156.6 nM. The same compound looks about 50 times weaker. That is why atp_um is a column and why IC50 values from different assay versions never share a column without it.

    1. Recompute Z’ for plates P-0003 and P-0004 from their control columns. In this example the plate rule is Z’ of at least 0.5 and an edge or drift effect under 20%. Which plates pass, and which messy rows fail[data]?
    Answer

    P-0003: 1 - 3 x (4800 + 1300) / (47800 - 4100) = 1 - 18300 / 43700 = 0.58. P-0004: 1 - 3 x (7200 + 1500) / (41000 - 4500) = 1 - 26100 / 36500 = 0.28[data][8]. P-0001, P-0002, P-0003 and P-0005 pass. P-0004 fails on both Z’ and its 27% edge or drift effect, so it is re-run[data].

    The messy table has four defects[data]. Row 1 stores the min-control spread as a CV of 22.5% (900 / 4000) in an SD column. Row 3 has no edge or drift check. Row 4 marks P-0004 accepted. Row 5 stores a Z of 0.35 from sample wells in the control Z’ column; the control Z’ for P-0005 is 0.79.

    1. Recompute KD for AB-0101 and AB-0102 from their rate constants. Then find the messy row whose KD disagrees with its rates[data].
    Answer

    AB-0101: 1.5E-04 / 2.1E+05 = 7.1E-10 M = 0.71 nM. AB-0102: 9.6E-05 / 4.8E+05 = 2.0E-10 M = 0.20 nM. Both match the good table[data][24].

    In the messy table, AB-0104 records 0.5 nM, but 6.0E-04 / 1.2E+05 = 5.0E-09 M = 5.0 nM, ten times weaker[data]. One of the three numbers is wrong, and the loader flags the row. The other defects: AB-0101 is in pM (710 pM is 0.71 nM, right value, wrong unit for the table). AB-0102 lost its antigen form while the orientation changed to antigen immobilised, where avidity can make binding look tighter[25]. AB-0103 has the unit typed into the value cell and no rates[data].

    1. Which CAR-T lots can be compared on potency, and what is their mean specific lysis? Derive VCN per CAR-positive cell for CT-LOT-2024-008. Then list what the messy table gets wrong[data].
    Answer

    Lots CT-LOT-2024-007, -008 and -009 were tested at E:T 5:1, with lysis of 62%, 55% and 71%[data]. Mean: (62 + 55 + 71) / 3 = 62.7%. CT-LOT-2024-010 shows 48% at 1:1 and is a different measurement, so it stays out of the mean[data].

    CT-LOT-2024-008 has 2.4 copies per total cell and 38.7% CAR-positive cells. 2.4 / 0.387 = 6.20 copies per CAR-positive cell, the value FDA recommends reporting[data][28].

    The messy table has four defects[data]. CT-LOT-2024-007 gives target-cell antigen density as MFI 1850, not molecules per cell. CT-LOT-2024-008 has lysis with no E:T ratio. CT-LOT-2024-009 gives VCN as 3.1 copies per µg of genomic DNA in the per-total-cell column, so the per-CAR-positive value cannot be derived. CT-LOT-2024-010 has no donor id. A loader should reject all four rows, not silently fill them in.

    1. Which titre for AAV-LOT-2025-001 is reportable, and how far apart are its two methods? Then list what the messy titre table gets wrong[data].
    Answer

    The ddPCR titre, 2.1E+13 vg/mL, is reportable. The qPCR titre, 2.6E+13 vg/mL, is characterisation data[data]. The ratio is 2.6 / 2.1 = 1.24, so qPCR reads about 24% higher here. That gap depends on the standard, which is why the qPCR row names it[34].

    The messy table has four defects[data]. Row 2 has a qPCR titre with no reference standard. Row 3 labels a titre vg/kg, which is a dose per body weight, not a concentration[31]. Row 4 has no method. Row 5 makes the qPCR titre reportable too, so a dose calculation could use either value.

    1. Which messy ICSRs are valid cases? For the serious case CASE-2024-0102, when is the report due under EU rules[data]?
    Answer

    CASE-2024-0101 has no identifiable patient, and CASE-2024-0104 has an unidentifiable product (“the blue injection”). Neither is a valid ICSR until follow-up fills the gap[data][53]. CASE-2024-0102 has all four elements, but its reaction is free text and must be coded to MedDRA with the dictionary version. CASE-2024-0103 has all four elements but no receipt date, so the clock cannot be set[data].

    CASE-2024-0102 is serious and was received on 3 May 2024[data]. The 15-day limit from receipt gives 18 May 2024[53].

    1. In the good lot-consistency table, for which strain is lot consistency shown? Then list the messy defects[data].
    Answer

    Strain 1: all three intervals lie within 0.67 and 1.5 (0.91 to 1.19, 0.85 to 1.11, 0.81 to 1.07), so consistency is shown[data][48]. Strain 2: Lot A / Lot C has a point estimate of 1.24 but an upper limit of 1.53, above 1.5. That pair fails, so consistency is not shown for Strain 2[data].

    The messy table has four defects[data]. Row 1 has no timepoint. Row 2 uses a wider margin, 0.5 to 2.0. Row 3 reads “Lot B vs Lot C” with no numerator; 0.93 for B / C is 1.08 for C / B. Row 5 marks the failing Strain 2 pair consistent.

    1. Repair the messy SEND body weight rows[data].
    Answer

    Row 1: keep 0.2453 kg in BWORRES and BWORRESU as collected, and set BWSTRESN to 245.3 with BWSTRESU g, the unit of the other rows[data][44]. Row 2: BODYWT becomes BW, the controlled term[46]. Row 3: USUBJID “1002” becomes TOX-2025-014-1002, unique across studies[42]. Row 4: BWSTRESN holds 270.4 as a number, and the unit stays in BWSTRESU[data].

    1. In the cross-reference log, which messy rows produce a wrong or failed join without raising an error? Then size the error if a lab converts a 10 ug/mL imatinib mesylate solution to uM using the free-base weight[data].
    Answer

    Four rows fail silently[data]. Row 2 links CPD-0102 to the salt record CHEMBL1642 instead of the parent CHEMBL941. Row 3 links the S-enantiomer to omeprazole, CID 4594. Row 4 returns nothing for the versioned Ensembl id. Row 5 stores NCT171912, which the API forgave but a local join will not. Row 1 joins correctly but cannot be reproduced, because it has no release or date.

    Molecular weights are 589.7 for the salt and 493.6 for the free base[77, 76]. 10 / 589.7 x 1000 = 16.96 uM; 10 / 493.6 x 1000 = 20.26 uM. Using the wrong weight overstates the concentration by 589.7 / 493.6 = 1.195, about 19%.

    1. How many guides support TGT-A in the good CRISPR table, and against which control sample? Then list what stops the messy rows from being grouped or compared[data].
    Answer

    Two guides support TGT-A: sg-00001 at log2 fold change -2.4 (FDR 0.001) and sg-00002 at -1.9 (FDR 0.004), both at day 21 against the day 0 pool[data]. Fold change and FDR are each relative to that named control sample[22].

    The messy table has five defects[data]. Row 1 stores the alias “tgt-a (alias)”, so when guides are grouped by symbol, sg-00001 will not group with sg-00002 and TGT-A seems to rest on one guide; map every gene to its approved HGNC symbol and group on the HGNC id[23]. Row 2 has no control sample. Row 3 puts a nominal p-value in the FDR column. Row 4 has no timepoint. Row 5 leaves the non-targeting control’s gene blank, so it cannot be told apart from an unannotated guide[data].

    1. Take, as this exercise’s rule, the minimum probe criteria one consortium set for epigenetic targets: potency under 100 nM, over 30-fold selectivity, cell activity under 1 µM and an inactive close analog. Which good rows of the probe dossier meet it? Then list the messy defects[data].
    Answer

    PRB-0001 meets it: IC50 8 nM, cellular EC50 150 nM, selectivity over 100-fold, and PRB-0001-NC is its inactive control (above 10000 nM in both assays)[data][21]. PRB-0002 also meets it: 25 nM, 400 nM and 60-fold, so it can serve as the orthogonal probe. CPD-0003 does not: its IC50 is 3.1 nM, but its cellular EC50 is 2800 nM and its selectivity only 12-fold[data]. TGT-A sits on a kinase panel here, and the consortium criteria were written for epigenetic targets, so a real programme writes down a rule suited to its target[21].

    The messy table has four defects[data]. Row 2 does not label the negative control and has no cellular value. Row 3 stores “95% at 1 µM” in the selectivity column, a single-concentration inhibition, not a ratio of potencies. Row 4 labels CPD-0003 a probe and has no cellular EC50 and no panel version.

    1. Give the mean RNA integrity and the mean encapsulation of the four good mRNA-LNP lots. Lipid lot LIPMIX-0043 is later found out of specification: which drug product lots are affected? Then list the messy defects[data].
    Answer

    Integrity: (91 + 89 + 90 + 88) / 4 = 358 / 4 = 89.5%. Encapsulation: (95 + 94 + 96 + 93) / 4 = 378 / 4 = 94.5%[data]. LIPMIX-0043 went into MRNA-DP-2025-003 and MRNA-DP-2025-004, both made from mRNA lot MRNA-DS-2025-022, so those two lots are pulled into the investigation[data].

    The messy table has four defects[data]. Row 1 stores a peak size, 4.3 kb, where the proportion of intact mRNA belongs. Row 2 has no encapsulation method. Row 3 has no lipid lot, so the LIPMIX-0043 question above could not be answered for that lot. Row 4 copies a drug substance value of 95% into the drug product row. mRNA can degrade during encapsulation, so the filled lot needs its own result[35].
