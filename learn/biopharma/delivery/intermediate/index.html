    Identifiers, units, traps and joins, turned into requirements, tests and signatures

    A number is readable only with the columns that define it

    • Potency: assay version and ATP concentration
    • Clearance: species, matrix and unit
    • Clinical: USUBJID and MedDRA version

    Source: Kalliokoski, Kramer, Vulpetti and Gedeck (PLOS ONE), 2013; Acta Pharmaceutica, 2019; US FDA (CDER, CBER), 2026; MedDRA MSSO, 2009

    Speaker notes

    Each of these columns becomes a requirement line and a load test. The FDA says an improper USUBJID often forces resubmission. Ask the room: which column in your current migration has no owner?

    An IC50 depends on the assay, so store its conditions

    • Competitive inhibitor: Ki = IC50 / (1 + [S]/Km)
    • Average potency as pIC50, not raw IC50
    • Single-point % inhibition is not an IC50

    Source: Kalliokoski, Kramer, Vulpetti and Gedeck (PLOS ONE), 2013; EMBL-EBI ChEMBL

    Speaker notes

    If Km for ATP were 10 µM (an assumption), 12.5 nM at 10 µM ATP would read about 631 nM at 1 mM. pChEMBL is computed only for dose-response types such as IC50 and Ki. Clearance needs its unit and species, and logD its pH. Ask: which of your potency columns would survive a vendor change?

    Biologic numbers carry their method and format

    • Antibody KD must equal koff divided by kon
    • ADC DAR: record the method, name one release method
    • Titer and endotoxin need units: g/L, EU

    Source: mAbs (Taylor & Francis), 2023; Scientific Reports, 2014; mAbs (Taylor & Francis), 2020; ACS Omega (American Chemical Society, open access), 2017; US FDA

    Speaker notes

    An antibody-drug conjugate is an antibody conjugate (class) that carries a cytotoxic payload to antigen-bearing cells (mechanism). One study found native SEC-MS within about 2% of HIC for one ADC across average DARs of 2.7 to 5.7; the defect is an unrecorded method, not a disagreement. Plasma stability is followed over time, separately per species. Avidity on an immobilised antigen can inflate affinity. Ask: does your load script check that KD equals koff over kon?

    A CAR-T lysis value needs its E:T ratio

    • Report VCN per CAR-positive cell, never per µg DNA
    • Target-cell antigen density: bead-calibrated ABC, not raw MFI
    • Donor id links the lot to its starting material

    Source: US FDA (CBER), 2024; Ikoma (Revista Brasileira de Hematologia e Hemoterapia), 2017; Poncelet, Lecompte, Bauters and Mullier (MDPI IJMS), 2026; European Medicines Agency

    Speaker notes

    CAR-T is an autologous cell therapy (class) using the patient's own engineered T cells (mechanism). FDA recommends VCN per CAR-positive cell, calculated from VCN per cell and % CAR-positive; vector testing includes sterility, mycoplasma, endotoxin and adventitious agents. MFI is in arbitrary units, and some studies report MFI assays as not reproducible within and between laboratories; calibrated beads convert it into antibodies bound per cell (ABC). In the table, CT-LOT-2024-009 at 71% (5:1) and CT-LOT-2024-010 at 48% (1:1) cannot be ranked together [D:cart-release]. Ask: who owns the E:T column in your schema?

    An AAV titre is readable only with its method and standard

    • Titre is vg/mL; the patient dose is vg/kg
    • qPCR needs its reference standard; ddPCR does not
    • One reportable titre per lot, from the release method

    Source: US FDA (CBER), 2020; Frontiers in Microbiology, 2019; Lock et al. (PubMed 24328707), 2014; CADTH (NCBI Bookshelf)

    Speaker notes

    FDA's 2020 gene therapy CMC guidance asks for a vector genome titre specification and qualified dose assays. In one study qPCR titres averaged 64 times ddPCR; correcting the qPCR standard cut the gap to 5.5 times. Ask: which column in your titre table names the method?

    In FDA's influenza guidance, lot consistency needs the whole interval inside the margin

    • Compare lots as GMT ratios, per strain
    • FDA influenza guidance: each lot-pair 95% CI within 0.67 to 1.5
    • Name the numerator lot, assay and timepoint

    Source: US FDA (CBER); European Medicines Agency

    Speaker notes

    The margin comes from FDA's guidance for seasonal inactivated influenza vaccines. In the messy table, Strain 2 Lot A / Lot C is marked consistent although its upper limit is 1.53 [D:vaccine-lot-consistency]. RNA medicines have an amount trap: a Spinraza vial holds nusinersen sodium equivalent to 12 mg nusinersen. Ask: does your load compute the consistent flag, or copy it?

    Clinical data keeps one subject key from form to analysis

    • CDASH collects, SDTM organises, ADaM analyses
    • Define-XML describes every submitted dataset
    • USUBJID identical across SDTM and ADaM

    Source: CDISC, 2023; CDISC, 2024; CDISC, 2021; CDISC; US FDA (CDER, CBER), 2026

    Speaker notes

    AETERM holds the verbatim term and AEDECOD the MedDRA term; AESTDTC is ISO 8601. Trials register under NCT plus 8 digits. Ask: where in your pipeline is USUBJID created, and who checks it?

    A safety case counts only with four elements and a version

    • Reporter, patient, reaction, suspect product
    • EU: serious within 15 days, non-serious within 90
    • MedDRA changes every March and September

    Source: European Medicines Agency and Heads of Medicines Agencies, 2017; European Medicines Agency, 2020; ICH; MedDRA MSSO, 2009

    Speaker notes

    The 15- and 90-day clocks are EU rules. EudraVigilance reporting uses the ISO ICSR format based on E2B(R3) since 30 June 2022. In the 2009 MSSO description, the September release changes only PT and LLT levels; March can change all levels. Ask: does your change-control process treat a MedDRA upgrade as a change?

    A batch result is judged against the effective specification and kept

    • Q6A: tests, procedures and acceptance criteria
    • Stability needs condition and timepoint (Q1A)
    • An OOS result is investigated, never overwritten

    Source: European Medicines Agency (ICH), 2000; European Medicines Agency (ICH), 2003; US FDA, 2022

    Speaker notes

    In the table, water 0.62% against not more than 0.5% is out of specification but unflagged in the messy version [D:cmc-batch]. The good version keeps the failing first assay run at 93.8% and links the 101.0% retest to the same investigation [D:cmc-batch]. A retest does not set aside an original result without an assignable cause. Ask: does your LIMS migration keep the first result and link the retest?

    Real-world data is visible only when mapped to standard concepts

    • Standard concepts go in _concept_id fields
    • Source value keeps the original code
    • Each distinct person_id is a different person

In the messy table, concept 0 halves a cohort from 2 patients to 1 [D:rwe-omop]. Ask: what share of unmapped rows will your acceptance test allow?

    Public joins use exact keys and record their provenance

    • Compound: full 27-character InChIKey to PubChem, ChEMBL
    • Target: UniProt accession to Ensembl to Open Targets
    • Trial: NCT plus 8 digits to ClinicalTrials.gov

    Source: InChI Trust (IUPAC); US National Library of Medicine, NCBI PubChem; EMBL-EBI ChEMBL; UniProt Consortium (Nucleic Acids Research), 2025; Open Targets; US National Library of Medicine, ClinicalTrials.gov; US National Library of Medicine, ClinicalTrials.gov

    Speaker notes

    A first-block InChIKey match means same skeleton, possibly different stereochemistry. Open Targets keys evidence by Ensembl gene and EFO disease ids. A preclinical programme has no NCT number, so "no record found" is the right output. Ask: what does your join do when there is no match? Cleaning writes a corrected copy and never edits the source.

    Each rule becomes a requirement, a test and a named signature

    • URS to FS and DS, each traced to tests
    • Effort matches risk: probability and severity of harm
    • Process owner accepts; QA approves; system owner controls change

    Source: ISPE, 2022; EMA / ICH, 2025; European Commission, 2011; US FDA, 2026; European Medicines Agency (GCP IWG), 2023; US FDA, 2025; European Medicines Agency

    Speaker notes

    What you will be able to do

    • Name the key identifier, the core units and the traps that make a right-looking number wrong for each modality and department your project touches.
    • Write a cleaning and migration rule set that normalises units, keeps censored values, replicates and dictionary versions, and never over-writes a source value.

    Learn

    A delivery lead does not need to run an assay. You do need to know which columns make a result meaningful, because those columns become requirements, load rules and acceptance tests. This module walks the data department by department, then turns it into the cleaning plan, the joins and the sign-offs you own.

    Small molecules and chemical probes: the compound key and three numbers that mislead

    A small molecule is a chemically made drug of low molecular weight. Its records start in a compound registry. Each registered structure gets an internal id, and each physical batch gets a suffix, as in CPD-0001 batch B01 in this module’s tables[data]. The public identity is the InChIKey: a fixed 27-character hashed signature of the structure, in three blocks joined by two dashes[1]. The first 14-character block encodes the skeleton (which atoms connect to which); the second encodes stereochemistry and isotopes[1]. ChEMBL addresses molecules, assays, activities and targets by ids that start with CHEMBL, such as CHEMBL25 for aspirin[2]. PubChem uses an integer compound id (CID) and accepts an InChIKey as a search key[3].

    Potency is the first number every project reports. An IC50 is the concentration of compound that halves an enzyme’s or a cell’s activity in one assay, usually in nM. A pIC50 is minus the base-10 logarithm of the molar IC50, so 1 nM is a pIC50 of 9[4]. Three traps turn a correct-looking potency into a wrong one.

    First, an IC50 is not a Ki. The Ki is the inhibitor’s own binding constant. For a competitive inhibitor, Ki equals IC50 divided by (1 + [S]/Km), where [S] is the substrate concentration in the assay and Km is the substrate concentration that gives half the maximum velocity[5, 6]. Every row of the potency table records ATP at 10 µM[data]. Suppose, only for the arithmetic, that the kinase’s Km for ATP is also 10 µM. Then CPD-0001’s IC50 of 12.5 nM gives a Ki of 12.5 / 2 = 6.25 nM. Run the same compound at 1 mM ATP, which is 100 times that Km, and the IC50 becomes 6.25 x 101, about 631 nM. Same molecule, same Ki, an IC50 fifty times higher. So the assay id, assay version and ATP concentration are part of the value, not metadata.

    Second, potencies are averaged in log space. The mean of pIC50 values, converted back, is the geometric mean of the IC50s, not the arithmetic mean[4]. Exercise 1 shows the gap on the illustrative table: about 70 nM against about 430 nM[data].

    Third, a percent inhibition at one concentration is not a potency. ChEMBL computes its standard pChEMBL value only for dose-response types such as IC50, EC50, Ki and Kd[4]. The messy potency table stores “42% inhibition at 10 µM” in the IC50 column, which a sort would rank as a 42 nM compound[data].

    In vitro DMPK (drug metabolism and pharmacokinetics) results say how fast liver enzymes clear the compound. Intrinsic clearance (CLint) from liver microsomes is reported in µL/min/mg protein; from hepatocytes, per million cells; scaled to the whole body it becomes mL/min/kg, and species differ[7]. LogD is the octanol-to-buffer distribution at a stated pH, usually 7.4; for a compound that ionises, logD changes with pH, so a logD without its pH cannot be read[8]. Potency and DMPK join on compound_id plus batch_id; note that CPD-0003 has only rat data, so a human-only filter drops it silently[data].

    A chemical probe is a tool compound used to test what a target does, not a drug candidate. For epigenetic targets, one published set of minimum criteria asks for in vitro potency under 100 nM, more than 30-fold selectivity within the protein family, profiling against a standard off-target panel, on-target activity in cells under 1 µM, and an inactive close analogue as a negative control; probes for other targets need criteria suited to them[9]. In the data, a selectivity fold must name its panel and panel version, and each probe row should point to its negative control[data]. Targets themselves carry one approved HGNC gene symbol and an HGNC ID[10], and a UniProt protein accession of 6 or 10 characters[11].

    Biologics: antibodies, ADCs and recombinant proteins

    A monoclonal antibody record starts from a clone id and an antigen id. Surface plasmon resonance (SPR) measures the association rate kon in M⁻¹s⁻¹ and the dissociation rate koff in s⁻¹; the equilibrium constant KD is their ratio, koff / kon, and SPR loses accuracy in the low-picomolar range[12]. That ratio is a free consistency check. In the messy binding table, 6.0E-04 / 1.2E+05 = 5.0E-09 M, which is 5.0 nM, but the row states 0.5 nM, so one of the three numbers is wrong[data]. Format matters too: a two-armed antibody binding antigen fixed to a chip can show artificially high affinity through avidity, so the antigen form and method are required columns[13].

    An antibody-drug conjugate (class: antibody conjugate; mechanism: an antibody that carries a cytotoxic payload to cells bearing its antigen) adds the drug-to-antibody ratio (DAR), the average number of payload molecules per antibody. Conjugation through lysines gives a wide spread of DARs in one lot[14]. The method is part of the number. One study compared native size-exclusion chromatography with mass spectrometry (nSEC-MS) against hydrophobic interaction chromatography (HIC) on cysteine-linked ADCs. For one ADC, nSEC-MS differed from HIC by 0.03 DAR on average and stayed within about 2% across average DARs of 2.7 to 5.7. For another, a formal equivalence test found the two methods equivalent[15]. Methods can agree, but only a recorded method and one named release method make two numbers comparable. The messy ADC table holds one lot twice, at 3.9 with no method recorded (row 1) and at 4.3 by UV (row 4), with no flag for which is the release value; the good table shows 3.9 by HIC is the release value[data]. Plasma stability is followed over time, separately in buffer and in human, cynomolgus monkey, rat and mouse plasma, and the results differ by species, so species and timepoint are mandatory[16]. A conjugate lot joins back to its antibody lot and drug-linker lot[data].

    For recombinant proteins, ICH Q6B treats purity, quantity and potency as attributes and ties each specification to an analytical procedure[17]. Titer, the amount of protein per litre of culture, is the classic unit trap: 0.12 g/L is 120 mg/L, and a mixed column sorts wrongly[data][18]. Endotoxin limits are set from the maximum dose that can be given within one hour and expressed in endotoxin units (EU)[19], so a result reported in ng/mL cannot be checked against them[data].

    Cell and gene therapies, and CRISPR screens

    A CAR-T product (class: autologous cell therapy, regulated in the EU as an advanced therapy; mechanism: the patient’s own T cells engineered with a chimeric antigen receptor) is made one lot per patient[20]. FDA guidance sets out CMC, analytical testing and comparability expectations for these products[21]. The release table records %CAR-positive T cells, vector copy number (VCN), viability, CD4:CD8 ratio, potency as % specific lysis, sterility and mycoplasma, for lots CT-LOT-2024-007 to CT-LOT-2024-010[data]. FDA recommends reporting VCN as the average number of integrated vector copies per CAR-positive cell, which can be calculated from VCN per cell and the percentage of CAR-positive cells[21]. For CT-LOT-2024-009, 3.1 copies per cell at 52.0% CAR-positive gives 3.1 / 0.520 = 5.96 copies per CAR-positive cell[data]. For the vector, FDA also lists endotoxin and adventitious-agent testing[21].

    Three traps recur. VCN per total cell, VCN per CAR-positive cell and copies per µg of genomic DNA have different denominators and cannot share a column[data]. A % specific lysis means nothing without its effector-to-target (E:T) ratio[data]. Antigen density belongs to the target cells used in the potency assay, not to the CAR-T lot, and when it is reported as mean fluorescence intensity (MFI) it is not a calibrated density[data]. Some studies report MFI assays as not reproducible within and between laboratories, and beads with a known number of antibody binding sites convert MFI into antibody binding capacity[22]. The calibrated unit is antibodies bound per cell (ABC). A 2026 review, whose first author worked partly for a maker of calibration kits, reports that these values reproduce between instruments and laboratories under set conditions, such as saturating staining, unchanged reagents and a maintained instrument[23]. The lot id joins release data to in vivo potency, and the donor id joins the lot to its starting material[data].

    Many gene therapies use an adeno-associated virus (AAV) vector, and their key release number is the vector genome titre, in vector genomes per mL (vg/mL)[24]. FDA’s 2020 guidance on gene therapy CMC asks for a specification for vector genome titre, gives qPCR as one method, asks that assays used to set the dose be qualified, and names particle ratios such as full to empty capsids as quality tests[25]. The method changes the number. In one study, qPCR titres for five samples averaged 64 times the ddPCR titres. The qPCR standard held 11.5 times fewer copies than its label stated; with the re-measured value, the gap fell to 5.5 times. Nine other samples, run against a standard measured by ddPCR, read 11% higher by qPCR[26]. ddPCR counts copies without a standard curve, so a qPCR titre needs its reference standard recorded and every titre needs its method[26]. The patient’s dose is a different number, in vg/kg[27], so it belongs in the dosing record, not in the titre table[data].

    A pooled CRISPR knockout screen switches off one gene per cell across thousands of genes, then counts which guides drop out. Tools such as MAGeCK rank guides and genes from read counts, comparing treatment with control and controlling the false discovery rate (FDR)[28]. The log2 fold change (LFC) of minus 1 means half as many reads. Each LFC needs its reference sample and timepoint, an FDR is not a nominal p-value, and genes are grouped by HGNC symbol, not by alias[data][10].

    Vaccines and RNA medicines: the comparison and the amount

    A vaccine lot-consistency study shows that three consecutively made lots give equivalent immune responses. In FDA’s guidance for seasonal inactivated influenza vaccines, the response is the haemagglutination inhibition (HI) antibody titre, and each pair of lots is compared as a ratio of geometric mean titres (GMT) for each strain. The two-sided 95% confidence interval of each ratio should lie entirely within 0.67 and 1.5[29]. A point estimate inside the margin is not enough; the whole interval has to be. So the assay, the timepoint, which lot is the numerator and the margin are part of the result, and the margin is fixed in the protocol once for every pair[data].

    An RNA medicine has its own amount trap. Nusinersen (class: antisense oligonucleotide; mechanism: a short synthetic strand that binds the SMN2 pre-mRNA and changes how it is spliced) is supplied as nusinersen sodium. Its label states each 5 mL vial contains nusinersen sodium equivalent to 12 mg nusinersen, so each mL contains 2.4 mg of nusinersen[30]. An amount column must therefore say whether it counts the salt or the free oligonucleotide. It is the same salt-or-parent question as for a small-molecule InChIKey.

    Clinical and safety data: one subject key, one dictionary version

    Clinical data moves through three CDISC standards. CDASH standardises collection on the case report form, SDTM organises the collected data into domains such as AE (adverse events), and ADaM builds analysis datasets traceable to SDTM[31, 32, 33]. Define-XML describes every dataset’s structure and is required by the FDA and Japan’s PMDA for each study in an electronic submission[34]. The FDA wants SAS XPORT version 5 files, one dataset per file, and a USUBJID (unique subject id) that is identical across SDTM and ADaM; an improper USUBJID is a common error that often forces resubmission[35].

    Inside the AE domain, AETERM holds the reported verbatim term, AEDECOD the MedDRA-derived term, AESTDTC the start date in ISO 8601 and AESER the seriousness flag[36]. MedDRA has five levels, from 27 system organ classes down to lowest level terms[37]. It is updated every March and September[38]. The March X.0 release can change all levels; the September X.1 release changes only preferred and lowest level terms[39]. Counts coded under two versions are therefore not directly comparable, and the version is a column, not a footnote. Trials are registered publicly under an NCT number: “NCT” followed by 8 digits[40].

    Pharmacovigilance (PV) is the science of detecting and preventing harm from medicines[41]. Its unit is the individual case safety report (ICSR). A valid ICSR needs four elements: an identifiable reporter, a single identifiable patient, a suspect adverse reaction and a suspect medicinal product. Under EU rules, serious cases go to EudraVigilance within 15 days and non-serious within 90[42]. Since 30 June 2022 that reporting uses the ISO ICSR format based on ICH E2B(R3)[43]. The case id joins intake, coding and submission; the MedDRA version travels with the coded reaction.

    Manufacturing, real-world and regulatory data

    Manufacturing (CMC, chemistry, manufacturing and controls) runs on the executed batch record in the manufacturing execution system, Level 3 of the ISA-95 model[44]. ICH Q6A defines a specification as a list of tests, references to analytical procedures and acceptance criteria[45]. ICH Q1A(R2) defines stability storage conditions; the general case pairs long-term storage at 25 °C and 60% relative humidity (or 30 °C and 65%) with accelerated storage at 40 °C and 75%[46]. A result is only meaningful with its lot, method version, specification version, condition and timepoint. An out-of-specification (OOS) result triggers a documented investigation, and an original result is not set aside by retesting without an assignable cause[47]. The batch id joins specification results to stability data.

    Real-world data comes from health records, claims, registries and digital health devices[48]. The OMOP Common Data Model standardises its structure and vocabularies[49]. In OMOP, only standard concepts go in fields ending _concept_id; local source codes are mapped to them[50], and condition_source_value keeps the original code[51]. A local code with no vocabulary entry can get a local source concept with an id above 2,000,000,000; such a concept is never standard, goes only in fields ending _source_concept_id, and is mapped to a standard concept[52]. EMA’s DARWIN EU network asks its data partners to convert to OMOP; EMA reports that it has onboarded 40 data partners since 2022[53].

    Regulatory data is organised by the Common Technical Document: five modules, Module 1 regional and Modules 2 to 5 common to all regions[54]. The FDA requires the electronic form, eCTD, for NDAs, BLAs and commercial INDs[55]. Product master data follows IDMP, five ISO standards that EMA runs through its SPOR services[56], and the FDA asks for a UNII to identify each active moiety[35].

    Cleaning as a discipline: the rules a migration plan writes down

    Tidy data puts each variable in a column, each observation in a row and each type of observational unit in its own table[57]. In practice that means a unit column next to every value, a replicate number on every repeat, and one table per assay, not one wide sheet per project.

    The rules below come straight from the traps above. Normalise units into a new column and keep the original, using SI prefixes (micro is 10⁻⁶, nano 10⁻⁹)[18]. Keep censored values such as “greater than 100 µM” as a qualifier plus a number, never as 100. Keep every replicate and compute summaries in a derived table, in log space for potency[4]. Record the dictionary version on every coded row[39]. Resolve identifiers against the registry before loading, and treat duplicates as a question, since OMOP counts each distinct person_id as a different person[51]. Add provenance columns: source system, extract date, mapping version and who ran the load.

    The rule that matters most for audit is that source values are never over-written. ALCOA++ asks that data be attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original and accurate, plus complete, consistent, enduring, available and traceable[58]. The MHRA’s ALCOA+ adds the first four of those five[59]. Under 21 CFR 11.10(e), audit trails record who created, changed or deleted a record and when, and changes must not obscure what was recorded before[60]. A cleaning script writes a corrected copy; it does not edit the original.

    Three worked joins to public sources

    Join one: internal compound to PubChem and ChEMBL by InChIKey. Compute the standard InChIKey from the exact registered structure of each compound, and record whether it describes the salt or the parent. Query PubChem’s PUG REST service with the inchikey namespace to get the CID[3], and ChEMBL’s molecule resource to get the CHEMBL id[2]. Accept only a match on all 27 characters. A match on the first block alone means same skeleton, possibly different stereochemistry, and goes to a chemist for review[1]. Compare activities in pChEMBL units, never raw IC50s from different assays[4]. Store the external id, database release, query date and match type next to the internal id.

    Join two: target to UniProt and Open Targets. UniProt’s REST API needs no login and includes an ID-mapping tool; a call for accession P00533 returns gene EGFR, HGNC:3236 and a sequence length of 1210[61]. Open Targets uses the Ensembl gene id as its primary target identifier, so map the accession to Ensembl first[62]. Its evidence is keyed by Ensembl gene and an EFO disease id[63], and its 2021 description combines evidence from 20 sources into one association score[64]. For many targets, use the data downloads rather than one query per target[65]. Record the platform release with the score, because scores change between releases.

    Join three: internal study to ClinicalTrials.gov. The internal protocol number is not an NCT number; keep a mapping table with both. The registry’s version 2 API returns one study when given its NCT number, with no API key[66]. Validate the format, “NCT” plus 8 digits, before calling[40]. A preclinical programme has no NCT number, and the correct output is “no record found”, not a near match.

    Operational systems you will be asked to deliver

    Many delivery projects are not research systems at all. In the US, the Drug Supply Chain Security Act requires an electronic, interoperable system that identifies and traces certain prescription drugs at package level[67]. So a serialisation project is a data project with a regulator at the other end: the package identifier is the key, and a broken trace is a compliance gap. Regulatory information management holds product master data that in the EU follows the five ISO IDMP standards, run through EMA’s SPOR services[56]. A preclinical lab system falls under Good Laboratory Practice (GLP) in 21 CFR Part 58, not under GMP[68]. So the first question on any lab system is which rule set applies. FDA treats data integrity as part of CGMP compliance[69], which puts the audit-trail and no-overwrite rules of this module into scope for manufacturing and quality systems too.

    The delivery lead’s part: requirements, risk, tests and signatures

    GAMP 5 gives the risk-based structure for computerised systems: user requirements (URS), then functional and design specifications (FS, DS), each traced to tests[70]. ICH Q9(R1) asks that the effort, formality and documentation of risk work match the level of risk, with risk as the combination of the probability and severity of harm[71]. The FDA’s Computer Software Assurance guidance applies the same risk-based idea, but is written for medical-device production and quality-system software, so state that scope when you borrow from it[72].

    Each rule in this module becomes a URS line and a test. “Every potency row carries assay version and ATP concentration.” “No microsomal and hepatocyte clearance share a unit.” “Every coded reaction carries its MedDRA version.” For a retrieval or prediction system, the acceptance criterion names the gold set, its version and the pass mark, and a fresh index or model version re-runs the test[data]. Retrained models and dictionary upgrades go through change control with a risk assessment and named approvers; the messy change log shows a MedDRA upgrade approved with no risk assessment and no tests[data].

    EU GMP Annex 11 bases the extent of validation on a documented risk assessment and expects the process owner, system owner, qualified persons and IT to work together[73]. Annex 11 names the roles but not the split. One workable split: the process owner is accountable for the URS and acceptance, QA approves the validation report, the system owner runs change control, and the delivery lead is responsible for traceability. The traceability matrix is the delivery lead’s daily artefact: each requirement needs a test case or a documented control, kept current as requirements change[58]. Annex 11 asks that an audit trail be considered on the basis of a risk assessment[73]; the MHRA expects audit trails to be switched on and not able to be switched off by users[59].

    Example data

    The potency table shows the compound key and the columns that make a number readable. Look for the pIC50 typed into the IC50 column, which reads as 7.9 nM, about 1.6 times more potent than the real 12.5 nM. Then find the value in µM in a nM column, the single-point percent stored as an IC50 and the second CPD-0005 row with no replicate id; the good table keeps both CPD-0005 replicates as r1 and r2[data].

    The CAR-T release table is per patient lot. Check the VCN denominators, the target-cell antigen density (a condition of the potency assay, bead-calibrated as antibodies bound per cell), and whether every lysis value has an E:T ratio and every lot a donor id[data].

    The AAV titre table holds one reportable titre per lot from the release method. Look for the qPCR titre with no reference standard, the titre labelled vg/kg, the titre with no method and the lot that ends up with two reportable titres[data].

    The vaccine table compares three lots per strain. Look for the ratio with no timepoint, the pair with a wider margin, the comparison with no stated numerator and the pair marked consistent although its upper limit is 1.53[data].

    The batch table joins lot, method version and specification. Find the result above its limit with no OOS flag, and the cell where a retest replaced a failing first result. In the good version the failing first run stays on record with its OOS flag, and the retest is a separate row linked to the same investigation[data].

    The ICSR table shows safety case intake. Look for the case with no identifiable patient, the reaction in free text, the missing receipt date and the product that cannot be identified[data].

    The OMOP table shows a hospital diagnosis code mapped to a standard concept. Look for concept 0, the missing source value, the text date and two patients loaded under one person_id. In the good table the local source concepts sit above 2,000,000,000[data].

    The traceability matrix links each user requirement to its test, the software version tested, the tester and the evidence. Look for the critical requirement with no test, the pass with no software version, the author who tested their own script and the test traced to a superseded requirement version[data].

    The DMPK table holds in vitro results that join to potency on compound and batch. Look for the clearance with no unit, the logD with no pH, the scaled in vivo clearance stored in the in vitro column and the blank species[data].

    The SDTM AE extract is a clinical record in submission shape. Look for the subject id in a different form, the empty dictionary term, the date that is not ISO 8601 and the serious flag that is not a controlled value[data].

    The serialisation table holds US package identifiers: NDC (National Drug Code, the US product number), serial number, lot and expiry. Look for the repeated serial number, the serial number that is too long, the missing lot and the expiry before shipping[data].

    Try it

    1. A dashboard shows “series mean IC50” for the five compounds in the good potency table. The table has six rows, because CPD-0005 has two replicates. Summarise CPD-0005 first, then compute the arithmetic mean of the five IC50s and the mean of the five pIC50s converted back to nM. Which should the requirement specify?[data]

      AnswerCPD-0005 first, in log space: √(55 × 61) = 57.9 nM (mean pIC50 7.237), n = 2[data]. Arithmetic: (12.5 + 480 + 3.1 + 1600 + 57.9) / 5 = 2153.5 / 5 = 430.7 nM. Log space: (7.9 + 6.32 + 8.51 + 5.8 + 7.237) / 5 = 35.767 / 5 = 7.153, and 10 to the power of minus 7.153 M is about 70.3 nM. The arithmetic mean is about 6 times higher because CPD-0004 at 1600 nM dominates it. Averaging all six rows instead would count CPD-0005 twice, so replicates are summarised per compound first. Potency is averaged as pIC50, which equals the geometric mean of the IC50s[4]. The URS line: “potency summaries are computed per compound as mean pIC50, then across compounds, and shown with n”.

    2. Join the messy potency table to the good DMPK table on compound_id plus batch_id. How many rows come out, how many distinct compounds, and what does the result say about CPD-0004?[data, data]

      AnswerThe messy potency table has 6 rows, two of them for CPD-0005 B01 (55 nM as r1 and 61 nM with no replicate id). DMPK has rows for CPD-0001, CPD-0002, CPD-0003 and CPD-0005[data, data]. The inner join returns 5 rows for 4 distinct compounds: CPD-0005 appears twice, each carrying the same CLint of 31 µL/min/mg protein, so a row count overstates coverage. CPD-0004 drops out: it has no DMPK record, which must be reported as “no record”, never filled with a typical value. The replicate fix is the one from level 1: give the second row its replicate id and summarise in log space.

    3. Rank the CAR-T lots in the good release table by % specific lysis. Which comparisons are valid? Then list what is wrong in messy rows 1 to 3.[data]

      AnswerCT-LOT-2024-009 at 71%, CT-LOT-2024-007 at 62% and CT-LOT-2024-008 at 55% were all tested at 5:1, so they rank against each other. CT-LOT-2024-010 shows 48% at 1:1, a lower effector-to-target ratio, so it is not comparable with the other three[data]. Messy row 1 gives the target-cell antigen density as MFI 1850 instead of the bead-calibrated 12000 antibodies bound per cell; MFI is in arbitrary units, and converting it to antibodies bound per cell needs bead calibration[23, 22]. Row 2 has a lysis of 55% with no E:T ratio. Row 3 puts 3.1 copies per µg gDNA in the VCN-per-total-cell column and leaves VCN per CAR-positive cell blank. The good table shows 3.1 copies per cell and 3.1 / 0.520 = 5.96 per CAR-positive cell[data], the form FDA recommends reporting[21]. Each needs a query back to the lab, not a guess.

    4. In the messy traceability matrix, how many critical requirements have audit-ready evidence, and what does row 3 hide?[data]

      AnswerNone of the 3 critical requirements. URS-001 has no test, result or evidence; URS-002 passed with no software version; URS-005 traces to requirement v1 while v2 is current, and has no evidence ref[data]. Row 3 hides a failure: the author USR-A ran their own test and recorded a pass, where the good table shows a fail with DEV-0007 raised[data]. Every requirement needs a test or a documented control, kept current as requirements change[58, 73]. URS-004 is covered by a procedural control, SOP-021 v3, which is acceptable when it is documented.

    5. An analyst counts patients with the standard concept STD-PLACEHOLDER-A (it stands in for a real concept id) in the OMOP table. What is the count in the good and the messy table, and what acceptance criterion would have caught the difference?[data]

      AnswerGood table: rows 1 and 3, persons 1001 and 1002, so 2 patients. Messy table: row 1 has condition_concept_id 0, so it is invisible to the query, and only person 1002 remains, so 1 patient[data]. Only standard concepts are used in _concept_id fields, so an unmapped code drops out silently[50]. Rows 1 and 2 also carry condition_source_concept_id 0, where the good table holds local source concepts above 2,000,000,000[data][52]. The messy table also shows 2 distinct persons where the good one shows 3, because two source patients were loaded as 1002[51]. Acceptance criteria: report the share of rows with concept 0 (here 1 of 4, 25%), require condition_source_value on every row, and reconcile distinct person counts with the source.

    6. Write three acceptance tests for loading the SDTM AE extract, using the messy table to show each would fail today.[data]

      AnswerTest 1: every USUBJID matches a row in DM in the form STUDYID-site-subject. Messy row 1 has 101-0001, so a distinct count gives 4 subjects instead of 3 and the row does not join to demographics[data][35]. Test 2: every AETERM has an AEDECOD coded with a stated MedDRA version. Messy row 2 is free text with no coded term, so it cannot be counted by preferred term[36, 37]. Test 3: AESTDTC is ISO 8601 and AESER is Y or N. Row 3 has 02/04/2024 and row 4 has “yes”, so a filter on AESER = Y finds 0 serious events instead of 1.

    7. Which rows of the messy vaccine table would let an inconsistent lot pass, and what acceptance test catches them?[data]

      AnswerRow 5: Strain 2, Lot A / Lot C is marked Y, but its upper limit of 1.53 is above 1.5, so consistency is not shown for Strain 2; the good table marks it N[data]. Row 2 uses a 0.5 to 2.0 margin, which would pass a wider gap between lots. Row 1 has no timepoint, so it cannot be matched to the primary timepoint in the protocol. Row 3 says “Lot B vs Lot C”, so 0.93 could equally be read the other way, as 1 / 0.93 = 1.08. Test: the load computes consistent as Y only when ci95_low is at least 0.67 and ci95_high is at most 1.5, using the one margin in the protocol, never a value copied from the row[29][data].

    8. How many of the five messy packages load without question?[data]

      AnswerNone. Rows 3, 4 and 5 each have a defect. Rows 1 and 2 share a serial number, so hold both until the repeat is explained[74].
