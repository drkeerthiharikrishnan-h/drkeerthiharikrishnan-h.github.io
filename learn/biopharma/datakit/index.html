Data kit

Hands-on labs on real public data. Each lab has a question from a named source, the dataset at its source, a notebook you can run and the published result to check your answer against.

Public resources what each one is, from an opened page

Structure repository. RCSB PDB is the US data center for the global Protein Data Bank archive of 3D structure data for large biological molecules. Source: RCSB Protein Data Bank, About RCSB PDB

Trial registry and results disclosure. ClinicalTrials.gov, to which study record managers submit registration and results information. Source: ClinicalTrials.gov (NIH National Library of Medicine), ClinicalTrials.gov results definitions page

Genomics portal. The cBioPortal for Cancer Genomics is an open-access, open-source resource for interactive exploration of multidimensional cancer genomics data sets. Source: cBioPortal for Cancer Genomics, cBioPortal FAQs

Statistical computing environment. R is a system for statistical computation and graphics, consisting of a language plus a run-time environment. Source: CRAN (The R Project), R FAQ

Clinical data repository. PhysioNet is a global platform for sharing biomedical and clinical data, software, machine-learning models and educational resources. Source: PhysioNet, About PhysioNet

Survey data. NHANES is a cross-sectional survey designed to monitor the health and nutritional status of the US population. Source: CDC National Center for Health Statistics, Obesity and Severe Obesity Prevalence in Adults: United States, August 2021-August 2023 (NCHS Data Brief No. 508)

Literature knowledge base. PubMed is a free resource supporting search and retrieval of biomedical and life sciences literature, with more than 40 million citations and abstracts. Source: NIH National Library of Medicine, About PubMed

Omics data repository. The NCBI GEO FTP site distributes GEO data as SOFT and MINiML files that incorporate experimental data and metadata. Source: NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus, GEO FTP site README

4Clinical 1 hand-off · 6 systems · 4 labs

Hand-off to Regulatory: SDTM, ADaM, Define-XML. Clinical study data for a submission. Tabulations in SDTM, analysis datasets in ADaM, both described by Define-XML. Sources: CDISC, SDTM, CDISC, ADaM, CDISC, Define-XML, US FDA, Study Data Standards Resources

  • SDTM. A common structure and format for study data, meant to ease collection, management, analysis and reporting.
  • ADaM. Standards for the datasets that statistical analysis runs on. Each analysis value can be followed back to SDTM.
  • Define-XML. A machine-readable description of a study's datasets, variables and controlled terms. Covers SDTM, SEND and ADaM datasets. CDISC states that FDA and PMDA require it for every study in an electronic submission.

This page covers S3, Athena, Glue, Batch and Quick Sight, the health services HealthOmics, HealthLake and Comprehend Medical with their scope statements, four public genomics and target datasets on the registry, two public NIH resources served from AWS, and AWS sample code that uses them.

Not on the map yet

A statement goes on the map only when its source was opened. These are waiting.

  • InChI at the discovery stage. The two pages tried (www.inchi-trust.org, iupac.org) could not be opened. Not opened, so not on the map.
  • E2B(R3) and eCTD as ICH standards. The ich.org pages returned a script shell with no text on 10 Nov 2026. Only the FDA scope is stated.
  • Which system of record is used at which stage. Six cells now have a source that names both the system and the standard (three company cells, three added later, two of them on the public-resources band). The other cells are empty, not negative.
  • Standards for ELN, LIMS, assay database, CTMS, eTMF, RIM, MES and QMS, and warehouse rows. No source opened yet. The platform pages suggest leads (FHIR and OMOP on managed cloud services for the EHR and claims row; SPL for RIM; recall records for MES and QMS), but each is a vendor or agency page about a product, not a statement that a company's system of record at that stage uses that standard, so no cell was written.
  • Standards for the public-resources band (structure repository, trial disclosure, genomics portal, survey data, literature knowledge base, omics data repository). Their definitions are sourced (systems.yaml), but no opened page ties a registry standard to those resources at a stage. GEO's SOFT and MINiML, ClinicalTrials.gov's data element definitions and PubTator3's BioC format are not in standards.yaml; adding them is a later decision.
  • AnIML as a standard. Proposed from a page that was opened (AnIML schemas 0.90 Draft). Not added, because its use list is a self-described adoption claim and its schema is a draft. Dataset-JSON was added to standards.yaml on 13 Nov 2026 from the CDISC page; no map cell uses it yet, because no opened page ties it to a company system at a stage.
