Drug shortage records (openFDA drug shortages endpoint; the FDA side only)

Drug shortage records from FDA, with status, shortage reason, dosage form, therapeutic category and posting dates, served as JSON by openFDA.

Dataset card CMC, Commercial · U.S. Food and Drug Administration (openFDA)

This card covers the openFDA drug shortages endpoint only. A European Medicines Agency shortages table was also considered; that side was not opened and is not described here. The openFDA page says manufacturers provide FDA most drug shortage information, that a shortage listed as current on the Drug Shortage Database means FDA is aware of the supply situation and is working with manufacturers to mitigate it, and that the data cover 2012 to the present and are updated daily. Fields include status, shortage_reason, availability, dosage_form, therapeutic_category, company_name, initial_posting_date, update_date and discontinued_date. On 12 Nov 2026 the API reported 1,586 records (last_updated 2026-11-12). Every response carries openFDA's disclaimer: do not rely on openFDA to make decisions regarding medical care, and assume all results are unvalidated.

Where
openFDA endpoint /drug/shortages (https://api.fda.gov/drug/shortages.json)
Access
Open, no login
Size and format
1,586 records in one zip file of 0.42 MB as listed in the openFDA download index (export date 2026-11-12). JSON (API responses and zipped bulk file).
Licence
Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication), unless otherwise noted. The openFDA terms say the data are public domain under CC0 unless otherwise noted, that some data may not be public domain (for example copies of copyrightable works made available to FDA by private entities), and that such works are marked by a warning in the documentation; no such warning was seen on the endpoint page opened for this card. The service is offered subject to the terms of service and to the FDA Website Policies, which were not opened. FDA asks for credit, for example "Data provided by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (https://open.fda.gov)".
Data dictionary
openFDA drug shortages searchable fields (reference also as PDF, XLSX and YAML)
Good for
  • Seeing how a regulator records a supply problem: status, reason, dosage form, therapeutic category and the dates a shortage was posted, updated or resolved.
  • Practising duration and grouping analyses on dated status records, knowing that the collection is updated daily.

Why this is not a lab: No published result located. The records change daily and no published figure for a dated snapshot was located: the clinical scout found none on 10 Nov 2026, and this card's author did not search further. The European side of the candidate was not opened, so no comparison across the two agencies is offered.
