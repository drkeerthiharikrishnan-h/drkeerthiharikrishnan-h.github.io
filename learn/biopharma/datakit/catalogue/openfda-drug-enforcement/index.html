Drug recall enforcement reports (openFDA, from the FDA Recall Enterprise System)

Classified drug recall records from FDA's weekly Enforcement Reports, with reason for recall, classification, recalling firm and dates, served as JSON by openFDA.

Dataset card CMC · U.S. Food and Drug Administration (openFDA)

The openFDA drug enforcement page says the API returns data from the FDA Recall Enterprise System (RES), a database of recall event information submitted to FDA, covering publicly releasable records from 2004 to the present and updated weekly. Recall information is posted in the Enforcement Reports once products are classified according to the level of hazard involved, and all recalls monitored by FDA are included in the weekly Enforcement Report once classified. The page's disclaimer says the data should not be used to issue alerts to the public or to track the lifecycle of a recall, because FDA does not update a recall's status (open, completed or terminated) after classification. Each record carries fields such as classification, reason_for_recall, product_description, recalling_firm, voluntary_mandated, recall_initiation_date and report_date. On 12 Nov 2026 the API reported 18,110 records (last_updated 2026-11-04). Every response carries openFDA's disclaimer: do not rely on openFDA to make decisions regarding medical care, and assume all results are unvalidated.

Where
openFDA endpoint /drug/enforcement (https://api.fda.gov/drug/enforcement.json)
Access
Open, no login
Size and format
18,110 records in one zip file of 3.79 MB as listed in the openFDA download index (export date 2026-11-10). JSON (API responses and zipped bulk file).
Licence
Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication), unless otherwise noted. The openFDA terms say the data are public domain under CC0 unless otherwise noted, that some data may not be public domain (for example copies of copyrightable works made available to FDA by private entities), and that such works are marked by a warning in the documentation; no such warning was seen on the endpoint page opened for this card. The service is offered subject to the terms of service and to the FDA Website Policies, which were not opened. FDA asks for credit, for example "Data provided by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (https://open.fda.gov)".
Data dictionary
openFDA drug enforcement searchable fields (reference also as PDF, XLSX and YAML)
Good for
  • Seeing what a quality and recall record holds: hazard classification, reason, product, firm, distribution and dates.
  • Practising grouping and trend tables on free-text reasons and coded classifications, knowing that recall status is frozen at classification.

Why this is not a lab: No published result located. No agency-published total or breakdown for a dated snapshot of these records was located: the clinical scout found none on 10 Nov 2026 (FDA pages on www.fda.gov refused its client), and this card's author did not search further. A tally by classification or year could not be checked against anything in print.
