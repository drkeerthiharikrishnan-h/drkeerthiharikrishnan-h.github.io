ClinVar weekly tab-delimited reports and a variation record (gene-level summary; VCV000007105, CFTR p.Phe508del)

Submitted classifications of human genetic variants in ClinVar (NCBI), seen through its weekly gene-level summary report and one variation record retrieved by E-utilities.

Dataset card Discovery, Clinical · ClinVar, National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI), National Library of Medicine

ClinVar's FTP directory publishes tab-delimited reports, among them gene_specific_summary.txt (3,499,711 bytes), variant_summary.txt.gz (458,376,982 bytes), submission_summary.txt.gz and summary_of_conflicting_interpretations.txt (2,080,408,602 bytes), each with an md5 file; the sizes are as listed on 12 Nov 2026. The README describes gene_specific_summary as generated weekly and archived monthly, based on statistics captured on the first day of each month, so it may differ from the web display; it gives one row per gene with total submissions, total alleles, pathogenic or likely pathogenic alleles, uncertain and conflicting counts. The copy downloaded for this card on 12 Nov 2026 is dated November 9, 2026 in its header. Its BRCA1 row gives 52,073 submissions, 16,101 alleles and 4,745 pathogenic or likely pathogenic alleles; its CFTR row gives 19,299 submissions, 6,511 alleles and 1,701 pathogenic or likely pathogenic alleles. The variation record VCV000007105, NM_000492.3(CFTR):c.1521_1523del (p.Phe508del), version 215, most recent submission 2026-11-07, carries 104 submissions from 91 submitters and a germline classification of Pathogenic with review status practice guideline.

Licence
NCBI Molecular Data Usage policy (no licence statement in the ClinVar README). NCBI places no restrictions on the use or distribution of its molecular data, but some submitters may claim patent, copyright or other rights and NCBI cannot give unrestricted permission on their behalf. As this site's practice, not as an NCBI condition, credit ClinVar and the submitters, give the file date or record version, and store no copy.
Data dictionary
ClinVar FTP README (tab_delimited section)
Good for
  • Seeing how variant interpretations from many submitters are aggregated into a gene-level summary and a single classified record with a review status.
  • Learning why a weekly report and the web display can disagree (statistics captured monthly) and why file dates and record versions matter.

Why this is not a lab: No published result located. The figures here are ClinVar's own counts and one record's classification, so a lab would only look values up or count rows. No analysis published on these files was opened for this card.
