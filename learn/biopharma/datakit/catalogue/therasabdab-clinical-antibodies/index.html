Thera-SAbDab: variable-domain sequences of antibody therapeutics with WHO INNs, their clinical status and structural coverage (OPIG, Oxford)

A database of antibody and antibody-derived therapeutics that have WHO non-proprietary names and public sequences, with their highest clinical stage, development status and matches to solved structures.

Dataset card Discovery, Clinical · Oxford Protein Informatics Group (OPIG), University of Oxford

The about page describes Thera-SAbDab as a database of immunotherapeutic variable domain sequences and their corresponding structural representatives in SAbDab, which harvests data from the PDB. It updates structural mappings with SAbDab weekly, detects exact and close sequence matches (95-98% and 99% sequence identity), adds therapeutics with a variable domain sequence whenever a new WHO International Non-proprietary Name list is released, and updates the clinical trial status of actively developed therapeutics from AdisInsight. The search page lets a user find entries by INN name, by attribute (format, target, highest clinical trial reached, development status, year the INN was proposed, structural coverage) or by sequence, and links the full list as CSV and XLSX, plus a separate list of INNs with no sequence. The database paper is Raybould and others, Nucleic Acids Res 2020;48(D1) (gkz827), as the search page gives it; the about page cites the same paper with the year 2019. No file was downloaded for this card, so no count of entries is given.
