MIMIC-IV Clinical Database Demo, version 2.2 (hospital and intensive-care records of 100 patients)

Real, de-identified hospital and intensive-care records of 100 patients, published openly on PhysioNet as a demo of the credentialed MIMIC-IV database.

Dataset card Real-world · PhysioNet; authors Johnson, Bulgarelli, Pollard, Horng, Celi and Mark, as given in the citation on the project page

The project page describes the demo as a subset of 100 patients of MIMIC-IV, the de-identified electronic health records of patients admitted to the Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center. It holds similar content to the full database and leaves out free-text clinical notes. The page says the 100 were the first subject_id, in random order, with an anchor_year_group of 2011 - 2013 or 2014 - 2016. The files are comma-separated tables in two folders: the folder listing of hosp shows 22 gzipped CSV files and that of icu shows 9. A further file, demo_subject_id.csv, lists the patient identifiers used to filter the full database. The full database needs credentialed access and is not used in this kit. The project page, the README, the licence text and the two folder listings were opened for this card; no data file was downloaded.

Where

Access
Open, no login
Size and format
15.4 MB (ZIP); 15.5 MB uncompressed in total, as printed on the project page. CSV (gzipped).
Licence
Open Data Commons Open Database License v1.0. The project page gives the access policy as "Anyone can access the files, as long as they conform to the terms of the specified license." The licence asks for attribution and share-alike. This card links to the files at PhysioNet and stores no copy and no preview.
Data dictionary
MIMIC-IV online documentation
Good for
  • Seeing what a de-identified hospital record looks like across admissions, laboratory results, prescriptions and intensive-care stays.
  • Practising joins across patient, admission and event tables before asking for access to the full database.
  • Comparing the native tables with the same kind of records in the OMOP common data model (lab mimic-omop-demo-achilles-counts, which is built on an OMOP conversion of an earlier demo release).

Why this is not a lab: No published result located. A lab needs a published result that a learner rebuilds from the files. For this demo and this version the opened sources print only its size, 100 patients. The project page also says MIMIC-IV has 26 tables, which describes the schema of the full database. The data descriptor paper of MIMIC-IV (Scientific Data 2023) does not mention the demo, and its numbers are for the credentialed database. The landing page of the MIMIC-IV online documentation, opened on 12 Nov 2026, lists the tables and prints no count for the demo; its other pages were not opened. A page or open-access paper that prints counts for this demo and version, such as rows by table, admissions or intensive-care stays, would reopen the lab.
