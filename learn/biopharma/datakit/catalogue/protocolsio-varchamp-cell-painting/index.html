VarChamp Cell Painting, a published wet-lab protocol on protocols.io (DOI 10.17504/protocols.io.36wgqnwkxgk5/v1)

A versioned, DOI-registered laboratory protocol on protocols.io for assessing the impact of missense variants on protein subcellular localisation with Cell Painting, from virus production to imaging.

Dataset card Discovery · protocols.io; authors Tanisha Teelucksingh and Florent Laval

protocols.io describes itself as a secure platform for developing and sharing reproducible methods, with versioned methods and history. This protocol, VarChamp Cell Painting by Tanisha Teelucksingh and Florent Laval (University of Toronto; Center for Cancer Systems Biology, Dana-Farber Cancer Institute), has the abstract 'Assessment of the impact of missense variants on protein subcellular localization'. Its PDF gives protocol status 'In development', last modified February 11, 2026 (the Crossref record gives 7 October 2024 as the date posted), keywords, and NHGRI funding (grant UM1HG011989). It has 17 numbered steps in sections for lentivirus production in HEK293T cells (96-well format), transduction of U2OS cells (384-well format), puromycin selection, mycoplasma testing, MitoTracker staining, fixation, staining with phalloidin, Hoechst and WGA, and confocal imaging on an Opera Phenix with a per-channel table of excitation, emission, exposure and power. The web page itself needs JavaScript; the steps were read from the protocol's PDF, which the page names as its citation PDF.

Good for
  • Seeing how a wet-lab method is captured as numbered steps with volumes, concentrations, timings and imaging settings, the kind of record an electronic lab notebook holds.
  • Learning how a protocol is versioned, given a DOI and licensed, and how its status (In development) warns that steps may change.

Why this is not a lab: No published result located. A protocol is a procedure, not a dataset with a published number, so there is nothing a notebook could compute and check. It is catalogued as an example of how a method is recorded, versioned and licensed.
