Take a medicine to patients

You lead a new medicine from a disease target to patients. At each stage you make the calls a real team makes, see what happens, and learn why. The numbers come from published sources, and the stage gates are rolled at real pass rates, so your drug can fail, just as most do.

Choose your role. Everyone gets the shared decisions; your role adds its own.
