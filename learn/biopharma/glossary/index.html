Glossary

119 terms in plain words. Each links to the public source it comes from.

AAV · Adeno-associated virus vector
A non-replicating virus shell used to carry a gene into cells. Zolgensma uses an AAV9 vector carrying the SMN gene. European Medicines Agency DiscoveryCMC and manufacturing
Accelerated Approval
FDA approval of a drug for a serious condition based on a surrogate endpoint, a measure expected to predict real benefit. US FDA Regulatory affairs
ADaM · Analysis Data Model (CDISC)
The standard for analysis datasets built from SDTM data, so every result can be traced back. CDISC Clinical developmentIT and data
ADME
Absorption, distribution, metabolism and excretion: how the body takes up a compound, where it goes, how it is broken down and how it leaves. Studied during discovery and lead optimisation. US FDA · Hughes, Rees, Kalindjian and Philpott DiscoveryPreclinical
ALCOA++
The attributes regulators expect of data. EMA lists them as attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original, accurate, complete, consistent, enduring, available and traceable. European Medicines Agency (GCP IWG) Quality and GxPIT and dataClinical development
Allotrope Foundation
A consortium building community standards so lab instrument data can be shared and analysed in one consistent form. Allotrope Foundation DiscoveryCMC and manufacturingIT and data
ANDA · Abbreviated New Drug Application
The US route for a generic copy. It usually relies on bioequivalence studies, which show the copy behaves the same way in the body as the brand, rather than new clinical trials. US FDA Regulatory affairsCommercial and market access
Annex 11
The EU GMP rule for computerised systems, in operation since 30 June 2011. It covers validation, data, security, change control and audit trails. European Commission Quality and GxPIT and data
Antibody-drug conjugate (ADC)
An antibody linked to a cell-killing drug. The antibody carries the drug to cells that show the target; trastuzumab deruxtecan, for example, carries a topoisomerase I blocker to HER2-bearing cells. European Medicines Agency DiscoveryClinical developmentCMC and manufacturing
Antisense oligonucleotide
A short strand of genetic code that changes how a gene's message is processed. Nusinersen helps the SMN2 gene make full-length SMN protein. European Medicines Agency DiscoveryClinical development
API · Active pharmaceutical ingredient
The substance in a medicine that has the effect. ICH Q7 sets GMP rules for making it. European Medicines Agency CMC and manufacturing
ATC · Anatomical Therapeutic Chemical classification
A WHO system that groups drugs by the organ or system they act on and by their therapeutic, pharmacological and chemical properties, in five levels. WHO Commercial and market accessReal-world evidence
ATMP · Advanced therapy medicinal product
The EU class for gene therapies, cell therapies and tissue-engineered products. Tisagenlecleucel is classed as a gene therapy product. European Medicines Agency Regulatory affairsCMC and manufacturing
Audit trail
A system-generated record of every relevant change or deletion, with who did it, when and why. Annex 11 expects audit trails to be reviewed regularly. European Commission · European Medicines Agency (GCP IWG) Quality and GxPIT and dataClinical development
Batch control (ISA-88)
A family of standards that gives common terms and models for batch manufacturing, used in pharmaceuticals. ISA CMC and manufacturing
Bioequivalence
Evidence that a generic copy behaves the same way in the body as the original medicine. It is the usual basis for approving a generic. US FDA Regulatory affairsCommercial and market access
Biomarker
A measurable sign in the body, such as a gene change or a protein level, used to pick patients or track a drug's effect. Programmes that pre-selected patients with biomarkers had about twice the likelihood of approval (15.9%) in 2011-2020. BIO, Informa Pharma Intelligence and QLS Advisors DiscoveryClinical development
BLA · Biologics License Application
The US application to market a biological medicine, such as an antibody, a vaccine or a cell therapy. Like an NDA, it must be sent in eCTD format. US FDA Regulatory affairs
BM25
A long-standing keyword search method that ranks documents by matching words. It remains a strong baseline in the BEIR benchmark. Thakur et al. (arXiv, 2021; NeurIPS 2021 Datasets and Benchmarks) IT and data
Breakthrough Therapy
An FDA programme that speeds development and review of drugs that may be substantially better than existing treatment. US FDA Regulatory affairs
CAR-T cell therapy
A patient's own T cells, modified to carry a chimeric antigen receptor (CAR) that binds a target such as CD19, then given back as a single infusion. Tisagenlecleucel is an example. European Medicines Agency Clinical developmentCMC and manufacturing
CDASH · Clinical Data Acquisition Standards Harmonization (CDISC)
The standard way to collect trial data on case report forms, designed to flow into SDTM. CDISC Clinical development
CGMP · Current Good Manufacturing Practice
The minimum rules for the methods, facilities and controls used to make drugs, so each product is safe and has the strength it claims. In the US: 21 CFR Parts 210, 211, 212 and 600. US FDA CMC and manufacturingQuality and GxP
ChEBI
An open, curated database and ontology of chemical entities, run at EMBL-EBI. EMBL-EBI Discovery
CHMP · Committee for Medicinal Products for Human Use
EMA's expert committee. It evaluates an EU application in up to 210 active days and gives an opinion; the European Commission then decides within 67 days. European Medicines Agency Regulatory affairs
Cohen's kappa
Agreement between two raters corrected for agreement by chance, from -1 to +1. Used to check whether an AI judge or a second reviewer agrees with an expert. McHugh (Biochemia Medica 22(3):276-282) IT and dataQuality and GxP
Computerised system validation
Proving and documenting that a computer system used in regulated work does what it should. EU Annex 11 requires validation; GAMP 5 gives a risk-based method. European Commission · ISPE Quality and GxPIT and data
Conditional marketing authorisation
An EU authorisation granted on less comprehensive data than normal because the medicine meets an unmet need. The company must provide further data, and EMA reviews new information every year. European Medicines Agency Regulatory affairs
Context of use
In the FDA's draft AI guidance, the specific role and scope of a model for one question of interest: what it models and how its output is used. Credibility is judged for that context. US FDA IT and dataQuality and GxP
CSA · Computer software assurance
An FDA risk-based approach to building confidence in software used in production and quality systems, with more testing where risk is higher. The guidance is written for medical devices (21 CFR Part 820). US FDA Quality and GxPIT and data
CSR · Clinical study report
The full report of one clinical trial. ICH E3 sets one structure that all ICH regions accept. European Medicines Agency Clinical developmentRegulatory affairs
CTD · Common Technical Document (ICH M4)
The standard layout of a marketing application, in five modules. Module 1 is specific to each region; Modules 2 to 5 (summaries, quality, nonclinical and clinical reports) are common to all. US FDA Regulatory affairs
CTMS · Clinical trial management system
The system a sponsor uses to run trial operations: sites, visits, monitoring and milestones. European Medicines Agency (GCP IWG) Clinical development
DARWIN EU
EMA's real-world data network, set up in 2022, with 40 data partners who convert their data to the OMOP model. Its coordination centre is at Erasmus MC in Rotterdam. European Medicines Agency Real-world evidence
Data integrity
Data that are complete, consistent and accurate through their whole life. FDA and MHRA both publish guidance on it. US FDA · MHRA Quality and GxPIT and data
Define-XML
A CDISC file that describes the structure and metadata of every submitted dataset (SDTM, SEND, ADaM). The FDA and PMDA require it for each study in a submission. CDISC Clinical developmentRegulatory affairsIT and data
Discovery
The first stage, where researchers look for a compound that could treat a disease and study how it is absorbed, how it works, what dose to use and what side effects it has. US FDA Discovery
eCOA · Electronic clinical outcome assessment
A system for collecting outcomes electronically, including what patients report about how they feel and function. European Medicines Agency (GCP IWG) Clinical development
eCTD · Electronic Common Technical Document
The electronic format for sending the CTD to the FDA. It is required for NDAs, BLAs and INDs for products meant for sale. US FDA · US FDA Regulatory affairsIT and data
EDC · Electronic data collection (electronic data capture)
The trial system in which site staff enter each participant's data on electronic case report forms (eCRFs). One of the computerised systems named in EMA's trial-systems guideline. European Medicines Agency (GCP IWG) Clinical development
EFO · Experimental Factor Ontology
An ontology used to annotate data in resources such as the GWAS Catalog, Gene Expression Atlas and BioSamples. EMBL-EBI Discovery
ELN · Electronic lab notebook
The electronic record of experiments, often linked to the LIMS. Raviteja and Gupta, JSS College of Pharmacy DiscoveryPreclinicalCMC and manufacturing
ERP · Enterprise resource planning
The business system for planning and logistics, at Level 4 of the ISA-95 model. ISA Supply chainCMC and manufacturing
Estimand
A precise statement of what a trial is trying to measure, including how events such as stopping treatment are handled. Introduced by the ICH E9(R1) addendum. European Medicines Agency Clinical development
eTMF · Electronic trial master file
The electronic version of the trial master file, the set of documents that shows how a trial was run. It must support identification, version history, search and retrieval, and be directly available to inspectors. European Medicines Agency (GCP IWG) · European Medicines Agency Clinical developmentQuality and GxP
EudraVigilance
The EU's single repository of reports of suspected adverse reactions to medicines, run by EMA. European Medicines Agency Pharmacovigilance
FAIR data
Findable, accessible, interoperable and reusable. Guidelines, not a standard, that put weight on machines being able to find and use data automatically. Scientific Data (Wilkinson et al.) IT and data
Fast Track
An FDA programme that speeds development and review of drugs for serious conditions with an unmet need. US FDA Regulatory affairs
FHIR · Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources (HL7)
A standard for exchanging health data between systems. Each piece of content is a "resource", such as a patient or an observation. HL7 International Real-world evidenceIT and data
GAMP 5
ISPE's guide to a risk-based approach to compliant GxP computerised systems, so each system is shown to be fit for its intended use. Second edition published 2022. ISPE Quality and GxPIT and data
GCP · Good Clinical Practice (ICH E6)
The international standard for designing, running, recording and reporting clinical trials, so participants are protected and the data can be trusted. The R3 revision's principles took effect in the EU on 23 July 2025. European Medicines Agency Clinical developmentQuality and GxP
Gene Ontology
A shared vocabulary for what genes do, in three parts: molecular function, cellular component and biological process. Gene Ontology Consortium Discovery
GLP · Good Laboratory Practice
The rules for running preclinical lab studies (in the US, 21 CFR Part 58). They cover staff, facilities, equipment, written protocols, reports and quality checks. US FDA PreclinicalQuality and GxP
GVP · Good pharmacovigilance practices
The EU rules for drug-safety work, in modules. Module VI covers how suspected side effects are collected, checked and submitted, including the 15-day and 90-day deadlines. European Medicines Agency and Heads of Medicines Agencies Pharmacovigilance
GxP
Shorthand for the good-practice rules: GLP for lab studies, GCP for trials, GMP for manufacturing and GVP for pharmacovigilance. Each sets who does what, which records to keep and how quality is checked. US FDA · European Medicines Agency · US FDA · European Medicines Agency and Heads of Medicines Agencies Quality and GxPPreclinicalClinical developmentCMC and manufacturingPharmacovigilance
HGNC symbol and ID
The approved human gene symbol and its stable identifier (HGNC:number) from the HUGO Gene Nomenclature Committee. Join on the ID, not on free-text names or aliases. HUGO Gene Nomenclature Committee DiscoveryIT and data
HIC · Hydrophobic interaction chromatography
A common release method for the average drug-to-antibody ratio of an ADC. A DAR value means little without the method that produced it. mAbs (Taylor & Francis) CMC and manufacturingQuality and GxP
High-throughput screening (HTS)
Testing very large compound libraries against a target, by machine, to find 'hits' that act on it. Projects screen between 200,000 and over 1,000,000 compounds. Hughes, Rees, Kalindjian and Philpott Discovery
Hit, lead and candidate
A hit is a compound that acts on the target in a screen. A lead is a hit worth improving. Chemists narrow hundreds of compounds to one or two candidates for preclinical studies. Hughes, Rees, Kalindjian and Philpott Discovery
IC50 · Half-maximal inhibitory concentration
The concentration of a compound that blocks half of an enzyme's or assay's activity. It depends on the assay conditions, such as substrate concentration, so two IC50 values compare only under the same conditions. Kalliokoski, Kramer, Vulpetti and Gedeck (PLOS ONE) · EMBL-EBI ChEMBL Discovery
ICSR · Individual case safety report
A report of one individual case of suspected side effects. In the EU, ICSRs must use the ISO format based on ICH E2B(R3) since 30 June 2022. European Medicines Agency Pharmacovigilance
IDMP · Identification of Medicinal Products (ISO)
Five ISO standards (11615, 11616, 11238, 11239, 11240) that define how to identify and describe medicines, substances, dose forms and units. EMA builds on them in its SPOR data services. European Medicines Agency Regulatory affairsIT and data
InChIKey
A fixed 27-character code hashed from a compound's InChI. The first 14 characters encode connectivity; the next block encodes stereochemistry and isotopes. So stereoisomers are expected to share the first block and differ in the second, though rare collisions have been reported. In a salt, each covalently bonded part is treated separately, so do not expect a salt and its parent to match. InChI Trust (IUPAC) DiscoveryIT and data
IND · Investigational New Drug application
The application a company sends to the FDA before it starts testing a drug in people. US FDA Regulatory affairsClinical development
Intrinsic clearance (CLint)
How fast liver enzymes clear a compound in a test system, for example in µL/min/mg microsomal protein. It is scaled up to predict clearance in the body, and values differ between species. Acta Pharmaceutica DiscoveryPreclinical
IRT · Interactive response technology
A trial system that randomises participants to treatment groups and manages the supply of study drug to sites. European Medicines Agency (GCP IWG) Clinical development
ISA-95
A standard that links plant control systems with business systems through a five-level model. MES sits at Level 3 and ERP at Level 4. ISA CMC and manufacturingSupply chainIT and data
KD · Equilibrium dissociation constant
How tightly an antibody or drug binds its target: the ratio of the off-rate to the on-rate. A lower KD means tighter binding. Very tight binders can be hard to measure by SPR. mAbs (Taylor & Francis) DiscoveryCMC and manufacturing
Knowledge graph
A network of entities, such as genes, diseases and compounds, joined by typed relationships. Hetionet, for example, links 47,031 nodes of 11 types through 2.25 million relationships from 29 public resources. Himmelstein et al. (eLife) DiscoveryIT and data
Label (prescribing information)
The approved text that says what the drug is approved for and how to use it. The FDA and the company agree it during review. US FDA Regulatory affairsMedical affairsCommercial and market access
LIMS · Laboratory information management system
Software that runs lab operations: tracking samples, workflows and assay data, often linked to an electronic lab notebook. Raviteja and Gupta, JSS College of Pharmacy DiscoveryPreclinicalCMC and manufacturingQuality and GxP
LLM · Large language model
A model trained on large amounts of text to predict and generate language. It can produce fluent text that is not supported by any source (a hallucination), so its outputs need grounding and checking. Ji et al. · Lewis et al. (arXiv, 2020; NeurIPS 2020) IT and data
LNP · Lipid nanoparticle
The fat-based particle that carries mRNA into cells in mRNA vaccines. WHO treats the degree of encapsulation as a critical quality attribute, and the particle-size test method must be stated. World Health Organization CMC and manufacturing
LOINC
Codes for lab tests and clinical observations, produced by the Regenstrief Institute. US National Library of Medicine Real-world evidenceClinical development
MedDRA
The international medical terminology, developed under ICH, used to code adverse events and other medical terms. Five levels, from 27 System Organ Classes down to Lowest Level Terms. MedDRA MSSO (ICH) PharmacovigilanceClinical developmentRegulatory affairs
MES · Manufacturing execution system
The system that runs plant operations, at Level 3 of the ISA-95 model. ISA CMC and manufacturing
MeSH
The US National Library of Medicine's thesaurus of medical terms, used to index and search PubMed. US National Library of Medicine DiscoveryMedical affairs
MFI and ABC · Mean fluorescence intensity; antibodies bound per cell
Flow cytometry readouts of how much of a marker sits on a cell. MFI is in arbitrary units and needs standardisation between instruments; bead calibration converts it into ABC, which can be compared across instruments when staining is saturating, the reagents stay the same and the instrument is maintained. Poncelet, Lecompte, Bauters and Mullier (MDPI IJMS) CMC and manufacturingQuality and GxP
MLR review · Medical, legal and regulatory review
A company's own check of promotional and medical materials against the approved label and local rules before use. In Europe, the EFPIA Code, a self-regulatory industry code, requires a medical doctor or pharmacist to approve each promotional piece. European Federation of Pharmaceutical Industries and Associations (EFPIA) · Veeva Systems Commercial and market accessMedical affairs
Monoclonal antibody
A protein made to bind one target. Pembrolizumab, for example, binds PD-1 on immune cells so tumours cannot switch those cells off. Given by infusion or injection, not as a tablet. European Medicines Agency DiscoveryClinical development
mRNA vaccine
A vaccine that gives cells the instructions to make a virus protein for a short time, so the immune system learns to recognise it. Comirnaty carries the code for the SARS-CoV-2 spike protein. European Medicines Agency DiscoveryCMC and manufacturing
MRR and nDCG · Mean reciprocal rank; normalised discounted cumulative gain
Retrieval scores that reward putting the right document near the top. MRR averages 1 divided by the rank of the first right answer. nDCG also weighs how relevant each result is, and counts lower ranks for less. Nogueira and Cho (arXiv) · Thakur et al. (arXiv, 2021; NeurIPS 2021 Datasets and Benchmarks) IT and data
MSL · Medical science liaison
A field scientist in medical affairs who exchanges scientific data with healthcare professionals and must not take part in promotional messaging. APPA, IFAPP, MAPS and MSLS Medical affairs
NDA · New Drug Application
The application to market a drug in the US. It holds all preclinical and clinical data, the proposed label, safety updates and other required information. US FDA Regulatory affairs
OMOP CDM · OMOP Common Data Model
An open community standard from OHDSI for structuring observational health data with standard vocabularies, so the same analysis can run on many databases. Current version CDM v5.5. OHDSI Real-world evidenceIT and data
OOS · Out-of-specification result
A test result outside the approved specification for a batch. FDA guidance sets out how such results are investigated. US FDA Quality and GxPCMC and manufacturing
Part 11 · 21 CFR Part 11
The US rule for electronic records and electronic signatures used for FDA-regulated work: records created, changed, stored, archived, retrieved or sent electronically. US FDA Quality and GxPIT and data
Pharmaceutical quality system
The management system a company uses to keep quality under control from development to the end of a product's life (ICH Q10). European Medicines Agency Quality and GxP
Pharmacovigilance
The science and work of detecting, assessing and preventing side effects and other problems with medicines. In the EU, reports go to EudraVigilance. European Medicines Agency Pharmacovigilance
Phase 1
The first trials in people, with 20 to 100 healthy volunteers or patients, to study safety and dose. FDA reports that about 70% of drugs move on; an industry analysis of 2011-2020 programmes found 52.0%. US FDA · BIO, Informa Pharma Intelligence and QLS Advisors Clinical development
Phase 2
Trials in up to several hundred patients, lasting months to 2 years, to study whether the drug works and its side effects. FDA reports that about 33% move on; an industry analysis of 2011-2020 programmes found 28.9%, the lowest of any phase. US FDA · BIO, Informa Pharma Intelligence and QLS Advisors Clinical development
Phase 3
Trials in 300 to 3,000 patients, lasting 1 to 4 years, to confirm the drug works and to watch for adverse reactions. FDA reports that about 25-30% move on; an industry analysis of 2011-2020 programmes found 57.8%. US FDA · BIO, Informa Pharma Intelligence and QLS Advisors Clinical development
Phase 4
Studies after approval, in several thousand patients, on safety and efficacy in wider use. US FDA Clinical developmentPharmacovigilance
pIC50 and Ki · Negative log of the molar IC50; inhibition constant
pIC50 puts potency on a log scale: 1 nM is 9, 1 µM is 6, and averages are taken on this scale. Ki is the inhibitor's binding constant; unlike the IC50 it does not depend on substrate concentration. EMBL-EBI ChEMBL · Kalliokoski, Kramer, Vulpetti and Gedeck (PLOS ONE) Discovery
Pistoia Alliance
A not-for-profit where more than 200 organisations across biopharma work together on shared problems such as data standards. Pistoia Alliance IT and data
pLDDT and PAE · Predicted local distance difference test; predicted aligned error
AlphaFold's confidence scores. pLDDT (0 to 100) scores each residue; under 50 often means disorder. PAE scores how sure the model is about the relative position of two parts, such as two domains. Varadi et al. (Nucleic Acids Research) DiscoveryIT and data
PMDA
Japan's medicines regulator. Like the FDA, it requires study data in the CDISC SDTM standard. CDISC Regulatory affairs
Post-market safety monitoring
Watching a drug's safety after approval, because the full picture builds up over months and years of use. In the US it includes MedWatch reports; the FDA describes Sentinel as a system to spot safety issues faster. US FDA Pharmacovigilance
Precision-recall curve
A plot of how many predicted positives are true (precision) against how many true positives are found (recall). On rare-positive data it is more informative than the ROC curve and its AUROC. Saito and Rehmsmeier (PLoS ONE) IT and data
Preclinical research
Lab and animal testing that checks whether a drug could cause serious harm before anyone takes it. It must give detailed information on dose and toxicity. US FDA Preclinical
Priority Review
An FDA designation with a goal of acting on the application within 6 months. US FDA Regulatory affairs
Quality risk management
A structured way to find, judge and control risks to product quality across the product's life (ICH Q9). European Medicines Agency Quality and GxPCMC and manufacturing
RAG · Retrieval-augmented generation
A language model that first retrieves passages from a document index and then writes its answer from them, so answers can cite their source and be updated without retraining. Lewis et al. (arXiv, 2020; NeurIPS 2020) IT and data
Re-ranker
A second model that re-orders the top results of a search by reading the query and each passage together (a cross-encoder). More accurate than first-stage search, but slower. Nogueira and Cho (arXiv) · Thakur et al. (arXiv, 2021; NeurIPS 2021 Datasets and Benchmarks) IT and data
Real-world data (RWD)
Health data collected routinely from many sources, such as electronic health records, insurance claims, registries and digital health devices. US FDA Real-world evidence
Real-world evidence (RWE)
Clinical evidence about a medicine's use, benefits or risks that comes from analysing real-world data. US FDA Real-world evidenceMedical affairsCommercial and market access
RxNorm
Standard names for generic and branded drugs from the US National Library of Medicine, so systems can exchange drug information. US National Library of Medicine Real-world evidenceIT and data
SDTM · Study Data Tabulation Model (CDISC)
The standard way to organise and format clinical trial data. It is required for submissions to the FDA and Japan's PMDA. CDISC Clinical developmentRegulatory affairsIT and data
SEND · Standard for Exchange of Nonclinical Data (CDISC)
The SDTM-based standard for nonclinical (animal study) data. CDISC PreclinicalRegulatory affairs
Sentence embedding
A list of numbers that represents the meaning of a sentence, so similar texts can be found by comparing vectors (cosine similarity) instead of words. Reimers and Gurevych (arXiv, 2019; EMNLP 2019) IT and data
SNOMED CT
A clinical terminology for recording information in health records the same way everywhere. More than 360,000 concepts, used in more than 80 countries. SNOMED International Real-world evidenceIT and data
Surrogate endpoint
A measure, such as a lab value or tumour shrinkage, that is expected to predict a real benefit to patients. Accelerated Approval can rest on one. US FDA Clinical developmentRegulatory affairs
UNII · Unique Ingredient Identifier
The FDA's identifier for a substance. Sponsors should get one early so study data links to one active moiety. US FDA (CDER, CBER) Regulatory affairsIT and data
UniProt
A free database of protein sequences with functional information, run by EMBL-EBI, SIB and PIR. UniProt Consortium (EMBL-EBI, SIB, PIR) Discovery
URS · User requirements specification
The set of owner, user and engineering requirements for a system or piece of equipment. It should be a point of reference throughout the validation life cycle. European Commission Quality and GxPIT and data
USUBJID · Unique subject identifier
The one identifier a trial participant keeps across every study and dataset in an application, identical in SDTM and ADaM. The FDA calls wrong use a common error that often forces a resubmission. US FDA (CDER, CBER) Clinical developmentIT and data
VCN · Vector copy number
For a CAR-T or other gene-modified cell product, the average number of vector copies integrated per cell. FDA asks for it per CAR-positive cell. US FDA (CBER) CMC and manufacturingQuality and GxP
WHODrug
A drug dictionary from the Uppsala Monitoring Centre, used to code medicines precisely. Uppsala Monitoring Centre PharmacovigilanceClinical development
Z'-factor
A quality score for a screening plate, from the means and spread of its positive and negative controls. Values between 0.5 and 1 mark an excellent assay window. Varbanov, Kuttler, Banfi, Turcatti and Dyson (PLOS ONE) · Li, Du, Chen, Fu and Harrison (Journal of Biomolecular Screening) Discovery
