    Each stage costs more, and each decision rests on joined tables

    • Capitalised cost per approval by area: $378.7 million to $1,756.2 million
    • Potency, exposure and safety sit in separate tables

    All figures are in 2018 dollars, from US data for 2000 to 2018. The beginner module covered attrition; this deck is about the records under each decision. Ask the room: which three tables decided our last Phase II go or stop, and do they share a key?

    A compound joins on its parent key, a target on accession and species

    • Imatinib base: CID 5291, CHEMBL941; the mesylate has another key
    • Human ABL1 is P00519; P00520 is the mouse entry
    • Open Targets keys targets by Ensembl gene id ENSG00000097007

    Source: NCBI PubChem; EMBL-EBI ChEMBL; NCBI PubChem; UniProt Consortium; UniProt Consortium; Open Targets

    Speaker notes

    ChEMBL lists CHEMBL941 as the parent of the salt record CHEMBL1642. The 14-character first block of an InChIKey cannot separate stereoisomers. In the messy table, no row is correct on every key, although each key column joins in half the rows [D:ext-id-join]. Ask: what share of our registry joins to ChEMBL on the full parent key, with the species stored next to every accession?

    Biologic numbers carry their method, species and units

    • KD equals off-rate divided by on-rate
    • DAR carries its method; plasma stability carries species and timepoint
    • Titer: 0.12 g/L is 120 mg/L

    Source: mAbs (Taylor & Francis), 2023; mAbs (Taylor & Francis), 2020; American Chemical Society, 2016; US National Institute of Standards and Technology

    Speaker notes

    One study found native SEC-MS and HIC statistically equivalent for average DAR, so the defect is a DAR stored without its method, or two DARs for one lot [D:adc-dar]. An avidity effect can make a bivalent antibody look far tighter on immobilised antigen. Ask: which DAR method is the reportable value for each conjugate lot?

    CAR-T potency needs its E:T ratio; VCN is reported per CAR-positive cell

    • At 5:1 lots average 62.7% lysis; the 1:1 lot cannot rank
    • VCN per CAR-positive cell: 1.8 / 0.452 = 3.98 for CT-LOT-2024-007
    • MFI is arbitrary units; beads convert it to antibodies per cell

    Source: US FDA (CBER), 2024; Poncelet, Lecompte, Bauters and Mullier (MDPI IJMS), 2026

    Speaker notes

    The FDA recommends the average VCN per CAR-positive cell, calculated from VCN per cell and the CAR-positive share. The lysis mean is (62 + 55 + 71) / 3 for CT-LOT-2024-007 to -009, and ranking by VCN per CAR-positive cell puts lot 008 above lot 009 [D:cart-release]. A lot without a donor id cannot be traced to its starting material [D:cart-release]. Ask: can every release value be traced back to its donor?

    Gene therapy, RNA and vaccine records each carry one number that sets the decision

    • AAV titre in vg/mL, with its method and standard
    • FDA influenza guidance: each lot-pair 95% CI within 0.67 to 1.5
    • mRNA lot: integrity, capping and share encapsulated

    Source: Lock et al. (PubMed 24328707), 2014; US FDA (CBER); World Health Organization, 2022

    Speaker notes

    In one study qPCR titres averaged 64 times ddPCR; correcting the qPCR standard cut the gap to 5.5 times. In the example vaccine table, Strain 2 Lot A / Lot C has an upper limit of 1.53, so lot consistency is not shown for that strain [D:vaccine-lot-consistency]. WHO notes that lot consistency trials may or may not be considered necessary, and may be particularly useful where manufacture is inherently variable. Nusinersen is an antisense oligonucleotide and Comirnaty an mRNA vaccine. WHO treats mRNA integrity and encapsulation as critical quality attributes; mRNA outside the particle is considered unstable. In the example mRNA table, 94% encapsulated leaves 6% outside the particles [D:mrna-lot-release]. Casgevy, made from the patient's own CRISPR-edited stem cells, has a 15-year registry-based follow-up. Ask: which number on our gene therapy batch record sets the patient dose?

    Trial data join on one USUBJID across every dataset

    • CDASH collects, SDTM tabulates, ADaM analyses
    • An improper USUBJID often forces a resubmission
    • AESER uses Y or N; dates use ISO 8601

    Source: CDISC, 2023; CDISC, 2024; CDISC, 2021; US FDA (CDER, CBER), 2026; PHUSE, 2022

    Speaker notes

    In the messy AE extract, one short-form USUBJID adds a fourth subject and "yes" hides the only serious event [D:clin-sdtm-ae]. Define-XML is required for each study in an FDA or PMDA electronic submission. Ask: who checks USUBJID before the data leave the CRO?

    A safety case joins to its terms only with the MedDRA version

    • Valid ICSR: reporter, patient, reaction, suspect medicine
    • EU rules: serious cases in 15 days, non-serious in 90
    • E2B(R3) format mandatory in EudraVigilance since 30 June 2022

    Source: European Medicines Agency and Heads of Medicines Agencies, 2017; European Medicines Agency, 2020; MedDRA MSSO (ICH), 2026

    Speaker notes

    MedDRA has five levels and 27 system organ classes. A peer-reviewed study found a 23.3% time saving drafting PV case documentation letters with one company's internal assistant, in one team of 3 over 4 months, 2 before and 2 after. Ask: does that measure speed, accuracy, or both?

    Real-world rows count only once mapped to standard concepts

    • OMOP keeps the source code and the standard concept
    • Concept id 0 means the code was never mapped
    • Local custom concepts sit above 2,000,000,000 and are never standard

    A batch result outside its limit is OOS, whatever the retest says

    • Water 0.62% against a limit of 0.5% is unflagged
    • A first assay run of 93.8% sits under 95.0
    • Keep run 1 and the retest under one investigation id

    Learn

    Why a leader needs the record-level view

    The beginner module set out the cost of a new medicine and how many programmes stop in Phase II. This module adds the cost per stage. Per drug, mean out-of-pocket costs are $21.0 million in Phase 2 and $89.3 million in Phase 3, in 2018 dollars[1]. Capitalised cost per approved drug ranges from $378.7 million for anti-infectives to $1,756.2 million for pain and anaesthesia, so the therapy area alone moves the stakes about 4.6-fold[1].

    A Phase II decision rests on tables: potency in one, exposure in another, safety in a third. If one number is wrong by a factor of ten, the decision is wrong and nobody sees it.

    This module goes through the data by modality family and then by department. For each, it names the records and identifiers, the core measurements with units, and the few pitfalls that make a plausible number wrong. A modality is named class first, then mechanism: “small molecule, kinase inhibitor”. You do not need to run the analysis. You need to know which question exposes a weak table, and whether an AI proposal stands on records that join.

    Small molecules and chemical probes

    A small molecule is registered once under an internal compound id, such as CPD-0001, and made many times as batches. Public registries give it other keys. Imatinib, a small-molecule kinase inhibitor, is CHEMBL941 in ChEMBL and CID 5291 in PubChem[2, 3]. Both can be reached from its InChIKey, a fixed 27-character hash of the structure[4]. The first 14-character block encodes the skeleton; the second encodes stereochemistry and isotopes; flag characters follow[4].

    Potency is usually an IC50: the concentration that halves the activity in one specific assay, in nM (nanomolar). It is not the same as Ki, the inhibition constant. For a competitive inhibitor, Ki equals IC50 divided by (1 + substrate concentration / Km), where Km is the substrate concentration at half-maximal rate[5, 6]. If a kinase assay runs ATP at its Km, the IC50 is twice the Ki. Run the same compound at a higher ATP level and its IC50 rises. So two IC50 values compare only if the assay, its version and its substrate level match[5, 7, 8].

    Teams often store pIC50, the negative log of the molar IC50, so 1 nM becomes 9[8]. Averaging pIC50 values gives the geometric mean of the IC50s, which is the right average for a log-scale quantity. Averaging raw IC50s lets one weak compound dominate the mean. A single-concentration result such as “42% inhibition at 10 µM” is not a potency and cannot be converted into an IC50[8]. A pIC50 typed into the IC50 column is a quieter error: 7.9 read as nM looks about 1.6 times more potent than the real 12.5 nM, small enough to pass review[data].

    Exposure data come from DMPK (drug metabolism and pharmacokinetics) assays. Intrinsic clearance from liver microsomes is reported in µL/min/mg protein, and from hepatocytes in µL/min per million cells[9]. Neither is the in vivo clearance in mL/min/kg; scaling between them needs stated factors[9]. LogD, the distribution between octanol and water, depends on pH, so a logD without its pH (usually 7.4) cannot be compared[10]. Potency and DMPK join on compound id plus batch id. A replicate stored without a replicate number looks like a duplicate and doubles rows in that join; CPD-0005 at 55 and 61 nM is one compound measured twice, summarised as 57.9 nM with n = 2[data].

    A chemical probe is a tool compound used to test a target’s biology, not a drug candidate. For epigenetic targets, one widely cited set of minimum criteria asks for in vitro potency under 100 nM, more than 30-fold selectivity within the target family, profiling against an off-target panel, cell activity under 1 µM, and an inactive close analogue as a negative control; probes for other targets need criteria suited to them[11]. A target-validation claim built on a compound that misses these criteria is weak evidence, however clean the chart.

    Biologics: antibodies, ADCs and recombinant proteins

    If your portfolio has antibodies

    A monoclonal antibody is recorded by clone id and antigen id. Its binding is measured by surface plasmon resonance (SPR) as an on-rate, an off-rate and their ratio, the KD: KD equals off-rate divided by on-rate[12]. An off-rate of 6.0E-04 per second and an on-rate of 1.2E+05 per molar per second give 5.0 nM, so a row reading 0.5 nM has one wrong number[data]. A bivalent antibody on an immobilised antigen can look far tighter than it is through avidity, so the assay format belongs in the record[13]. Mixed pM and nM units break any sort by value[14].

    An antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) is an antibody carrying a cytotoxic payload through a linker. Trastuzumab deruxtecan, an ADC directed at HER2, is one approved example[15]. Three lots meet in one product: the antibody lot, the drug-linker lot and the conjugate lot. The drug-to-antibody ratio (DAR) is the average number of payload molecules per antibody. Methods can agree: one study found native size-exclusion chromatography with mass spectrometry (SEC-MS) and hydrophobic interaction chromatography (HIC) statistically equivalent for average DAR across several ADCs[16]. The defect to look for is a DAR stored without its method, or two DARs for one lot with no flag for the reportable one[data]. Lysine coupling gives a wide spread of DARs[17]. Plasma stability is measured as DAR retained over time, separately in buffer and in human, monkey, mouse and rat plasma, so a stability number without species and timepoint means little[18].

    Recombinant proteins are recorded by construct id and batch. Titer, the amount made per litre of culture, mixes mg/L and g/L in many tables; 0.12 g/L is 120 mg/L[14]. Endotoxin limits are set in endotoxin units (EU), not as a mass concentration[19]. For biotechnology products, ICH Q6B links each specification to an analytical procedure and names purity, quantity and potency among the attributes[20].

    Cell therapies and CRISPR screens

    A CAR-T product is a cell therapy: a patient’s own T cells engineered to carry a chimeric antigen receptor (CAR) that recognises a tumour antigen[21]. Each lot links to a donor and an apheresis collection. For a product made from one patient’s cells, that chain of identity is the join that matters most; a lot without a donor id cannot be traced to its starting material[data].

    The example release table holds the share of T cells carrying the CAR (%), vector copy number (VCN), viability (%), a potency assay, sterility and mycoplasma[data]. The FDA’s CAR-T guidance cites sterility and mycoplasma testing, and its lot release expectations depend on the development phase[22]. For integrating vectors it recommends reporting the average VCN per CAR-positive cell, which can be calculated from VCN per cell and the percentage of CAR-positive cells[22].

    Three pitfalls recur. Percent specific lysis cannot be compared without the effector-to-target (E:T) ratio and timepoint, so both belong in the potency result[data]. VCN per µg of genomic DNA is a third denominator: it is neither VCN per cell nor VCN per CAR-positive cell[data]. Antigen density reported as a mean fluorescence intensity (MFI) is in arbitrary units and needs standardisation between instruments; calibration beads convert it into antibodies bound per cell. A 2026 review, whose first author worked partly for a maker of calibration kits, reports that this unit reproduces across instruments and laboratories under set conditions, such as saturating staining and unchanged reagents[23, 24].

    If your portfolio uses CRISPR screens

    A pooled CRISPR knockout screen measures how the abundance of each guide RNA changes over time in culture. Software such as MAGeCK ranks genes from read counts in treated versus control samples and controls the false discovery rate (FDR)[25]. A log2 fold change (LFC) of minus 1 means the guide’s abundance is half of what it was in the reference sample. Store which sample is the reference, the plasmid pool or day 0, because the fold change is relative to it; a nominal p-value written in the FDR column overstates confidence[data]. Each gene has one approved HGNC symbol; aliases split one gene into two rows[26].

    Gene therapy, RNA medicines and vaccines

    A gene therapy delivers genetic material in a vector. Onasemnogene abeparvovec (Zolgensma) is an adeno-associated virus (AAV) vector that delivers a working SMN gene; it is given once by infusion, with the dose in vector genomes per kilogram (vg/kg)[27]. The batch number that sets the dose is the vector genome titre, in vector genomes per mL[28]. Droplet digital PCR (ddPCR) counts copies directly; qPCR reads the titre off a standard curve, so the standard is part of the number[29]. In one study, qPCR titres for five samples averaged 64 times the ddPCR titres. The qPCR standard held 11.5 times fewer copies than its label stated; with the re-measured value, the gap fell to 5.5 times. Nine other samples, run against a standard measured by ddPCR, read 11% higher by qPCR[29]. A titre therefore carries its method, its target region and, for qPCR, its standard[data].

    Some of these products are watched for years. Casgevy is made individually from each patient’s own blood stem cells, which are edited with CRISPR/Cas9. It has a 15-year registry-based follow-up of long-term effects[30]. The patient, the lot and the follow-up record must stay joinable for that whole period.

    RNA medicines act through a sequence. Nusinersen (Spinraza) is an antisense oligonucleotide acting on SMN2 and injected into the spinal fluid[31]. Comirnaty is an mRNA vaccine that encodes the coronavirus spike protein and is given into a muscle[32]. Their lots, specifications and trials follow the CMC and clinical records described below.

    An mRNA vaccine adds two release numbers. WHO treats mRNA integrity as a critical quality attribute for release: the share of the mRNA that is the correct size and carries its 5′ cap and poly(A) tail[33]. The share of mRNA encapsulated in the lipid nanoparticle (LNP) is another critical quality attribute, because mRNA outside the particle is considered unstable[33]. WHO also asks that the method for particle size be stated, because the result depends on the method[33]. So an mRNA lot record carries integrity, capping and encapsulation, each with its method, and the drug product lot keeps the id of the mRNA lot it was made from[data].

    Vaccines add an immunogenicity record. A geometric mean titre (GMT) is a group’s average antibody titre, calculated by multiplying all values and taking the nth root, where n is the number of subjects with data[34]. A clinical lot consistency study tests whether three consecutively made lots give equivalent immune responses. FDA influenza guidance compares each pair of lots by the ratio of their GMTs, for each strain, and asks that the whole two-sided 95% confidence interval lie within 0.67 and 1.5[35]. WHO notes that such trials may or may not be considered necessary, and may be particularly useful where manufacture is inherently variable[34].

    Clinical, safety and real-world data

    Clinical data follow one chain. CDASH sets how data are collected, SDTM how they are tabulated for submission, and ADaM how analysis datasets trace back to SDTM[36, 37, 38]. Define-XML describes every dataset and is required by FDA and PMDA for each study in an electronic submission[39]. Animal studies have their own version of the same idea: SEND, built on SDTM, sets the structure of nonclinical datasets such as general toxicology studies for submission to a regulator[40]. The subject key is USUBJID. It must be unique per subject across all studies in an application and identical in SDTM and ADaM; an improper USUBJID often forces a resubmission[41]. In the adverse event (AE) domain, AETERM holds the reported words, AEDECOD the MedDRA term, AESTDTC an ISO 8601 date and AESER a Y or N flag[42].

    Pharmacovigilance (PV) is the monitoring of side effects once a medicine is in use. Its unit is the individual case safety report (ICSR). A valid ICSR needs four elements: an identifiable reporter, a single identifiable patient, a suspect reaction and a suspect medicine[43]. Under the EU rules, serious cases are reported within 15 days and non-serious within 90[43]. EU reporting has used the ISO format based on ICH E2B(R3) since 30 June 2022[44]. Reactions are coded in MedDRA, a five-level hierarchy from 27 system organ classes down to lowest level terms[45]. MedDRA is versioned, so a case id joins to its terms only together with the dictionary version used[46].

    Real-world data (RWD) come from health records, claims, registries and devices[47]. The OMOP common data model gives them one structure and standard vocabularies[48]. Each row keeps the original code in a source-value field and the mapped standard concept in a concept-id field; only standard concepts go in fields ending in _CONCEPT_ID[49, 50]. A concept id of 0 marks a code that was never mapped, and that row is invisible to any standard analysis[49]. A local code with no vocabulary entry gets a custom concept id above 2,000,000,000; it is never standard, sits only in source-concept fields and maps to a standard concept[51]. EMA’s DARWIN EU network draws on about 40 data partners that convert their data to OMOP[52].

    CMC and regulatory records

    CMC (chemistry, manufacturing and controls) records centre on the batch, also called the lot. The executed batch record says how it was made; the LIMS holds its test results. A specification is the list of tests, references to analytical procedures and acceptance criteria that a batch must meet[53]. A result outside its criterion is out of specification (OOS) and needs a documented investigation. A passing retest does not cancel the first result unless a laboratory error is documented[54]. So a good batch table keeps the failing first run and the retest as two rows under one investigation id[data]. Stability studies under ICH Q1A(R2) test primary batches over time at long-term and accelerated conditions, such as 40 °C and 75% relative humidity for six months[55]. Batch is the join across release results, stability pulls and, for a cell product, the patient it went to.

    Two habits protect all of this. Data should be attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original and accurate, plus complete, consistent, enduring, available and traceable: ten attributes, called ALCOA++[56]. Audit trails stay switched on, users cannot switch them off, and their review is documented and based on risk[57].

    Regulatory records gather everything into the Common Technical Document (CTD). Quality data sit in Module 3, nonclinical in Module 4 and clinical in Module 5[58]. Submissions go as eCTD, required at the FDA for NDAs, BLAs and commercial INDs[59]. Product identity across regulators follows IDMP; EMA implements the five ISO IDMP standards through its SPOR data services[60]. The FDA asks for a UNII code for each active moiety so all study data point to one substance[41].

    Commercial, medical and supply records

    Partners often sell into the functions closest to the market, and their records follow different rules. Supply chain records identify each pack. In the US, DSCSA sets up an electronic, interoperable system to identify and trace certain prescription drugs at package level[61]. In the EU, the Falsified Medicines Directive requires a unique identifier and an anti-tampering device on the outer pack, with the detailed rules applying since 9 February 2019[62]. The pack serial number is the join key, not the batch alone.

    Medical affairs gives health professionals accurate, unbiased scientific information. Its medical science liaisons do scientific exchange, not promotion[63]. For any tool that drafts or answers for medical affairs, the first question is which side of that line its output falls on, and who reviews it.

    Market access prepares the evidence that payers and health technology bodies assess. Since 12 January 2025, new cancer medicines and advanced therapies in the EU are subject to a joint clinical assessment of their relative effects on health outcomes[64]. That assessment draws on the same trial records described above, so a broken USUBJID or an unmapped term reaches the payer dossier too.

    Patient data carry legal limits. Under GDPR, data concerning health and genetic data are special categories; processing them is prohibited unless one of the listed exceptions applies[65]. A vendor that processes such data for a company is a processor. It needs a written contract that sets out the subject matter, duration, nature and purpose, the types of personal data and the categories of people, and it needs written authorisation before it engages another processor[66].

    Cleaning rules and a worked join to public records

    Data cleaning is a discipline with rules. Tidy data has one variable per column, one observation per row and one table per type of unit[67]. Store each value with its unit and convert with SI prefixes: 0.48 µM is 480 nM[14]. Keep censored values censored. ChEMBL holds 105 IC50 records for imatinib whose relation is “greater than”, such as one above 30,000 nM; those records carry no pChEMBL value[2]. A careful team stores the qualifier in its own column and never saves the bare number as if it were measured. Keep each replicate with a replicate number and average in log space. Record the dictionary version next to every coded term[46]. De-duplicate on identity, not on look: two patients loaded under one person_id are a defect, not a duplicate[49]. Add provenance columns (who, when, instrument, method version), and never overwrite a source value; correct it in a new field with an audit trail[56, 68].

    Now the join. Suppose the registry holds CPD-0101, the reference compound imatinib, as two batches: B01 free base and B02 mesylate salt. The free-base structure gives InChIKey KTUFNOKKBVMGRW-UHFFFAOYSA-N, which resolves to PubChem CID 5291 and ChEMBL CHEMBL941[3, 2]. The salt gives a different key, YLMAHDNUQAMNNX-UHFFFAOYSA-N, which resolves to CID 123596 and CHEMBL1642; ChEMBL lists CHEMBL941 as its parent[69, 70]. The molecular weights differ too: 493.6 for the free base and 589.7 for the salt[3, 69]. A 1 mg/mL solution is 2.03 mM as free base but 1.70 mM as salt, about 16% less. A careful team joins on the parent key and records the salt separately.

    The target joins next. ABL1 is UniProt P00519 in human, and P00520 is the same protein name in mouse[71, 72]. A UniProt accession has 6 or 10 characters[73]. The human entry cross-references Ensembl gene ENSG00000097007, the key Open Targets uses; that id returns ABL1 there, and CHEMBL941 returns imatinib[71, 74]. For a whole portfolio, the Open Targets documentation points to bulk downloads rather than one query per target[75].

    The trial joins last. Finerenone, a small-molecule mineralocorticoid receptor blocker approved in the EU as Kerendia, was tested in FIDELIO-DKD[76]. That registry record, NCT02540993, is a completed Phase 3 study with 5,734 participants[77]. Internal study codes never appear in the registry. The link is a mapping table, kept with its source and date, from each internal study id to its NCT number.

    Reading an AI proposal with this map

    Evidence comes in grades. A peer-reviewed study of one company’s internal language-model assistant, in one team of 3 over 4 months (2 months before and 2 months after, with no separate control group), found a 23.3% time saving (95% CI 13.8 to 32.8%) in drafting pharmacovigilance case documentation letters[78]. That measures drafting time on one task in one small team, not coding accuracy. A company report that first drafts of clinical study reports fell from 2 to 3 weeks to 3 to 4 days is company-reported[79]. An announcement that five companies fine-tuned a structure model on more than 20,000 private structures without pooling them was published by the platform company[80]. Each grade supports a different decision.

    Five questions test any proposal against the map above. First: which decision, which department and which system of record? Second: which identifiers join the records, and what share of rows join on every key, not just one? Third: what is the evaluation set? The FDA’s draft asks that test data be independent of development data and not shown during training[81]. Fourth: what is the context of use and the cost of a wrong answer? The FDA’s January 2025 draft, which is not binding, sets model risk from the model’s influence on a decision and the consequence of that decision[81]. For pivotal trials, EMA expects models and pre-processing to be frozen and documented before unblinding; in pharmacovigilance it allows a more flexible approach, including incremental learning, if performance is monitored and documented[82]. Fifth: who keeps it valid when an assay version, a method or a MedDRA version changes[46]?

    In January 2026 EMA and FDA jointly published ten guiding principles of good AI practice in drug development. They are high-level principles meant to inform future guidance and standards; they ask, among other things, for a clear context of use and for data provenance and processing steps documented in a traceable way[83]. Older machine-learning principles from 2021 were written for medical devices, not for medicines[84].

    The same map frames build, buy or partner. Build where the identifiers and judgement are internal, such as the compound registry and assay versions. Buy where the target format is a shared standard fixed by others, such as E2B(R3), SDTM or OMOP[44, 37, 48]. Partner where data cannot be pooled. In every case the sponsor keeps responsibility for work done by a vendor[56].

    Try it

    1. In the messy external-join table, how many of the four rows join correctly on every key (compound, PubChem, ChEMBL, target)? What does a per-column join rate hide?[data]

      Answer Row 1 joins the compound to CHEMBL941 but carries the mouse accession P00520, so its target is wrong. Row 2 joins the target but its salt key lands on CID 123596 and CHEMBL1642, not the parent. Row 3 has only the 14-character first block, so an exact-key join finds nothing. Row 4 joins the compound but its target “MR” has no accession or Ensembl id[data][72, 70, 4]. Compound joins to the parent: 2 of 4 rows (1 and 4), 50%. Target joins correctly: 2 of 4 (2 and 3), 50%. Rows correct on every key: 0 of 4. Two 50% field rates hide a 0% row rate.

    2. A team shows you the good CAR-T table and ranks the four lots on potency to support a go decision. Would you rely on that ranking, and what single question exposes the problem?[data]

      Answer Refuse the ranking as shown, and ask: “Were all four lots tested at the same E:T ratio?” CT-LOT-2024-007, -008 and -009 were tested at 5:1: (62 + 55 + 71) / 3 = 62.7%[data]. CT-LOT-2024-010 shows 48% at 1:1. At a lower E:T ratio there are fewer effector cells per target cell, so the number is not comparable; it cannot be ranked last on this evidence. In the messy table CT-LOT-2024-008 has no ratio at all, so its 55% cannot be placed either[data].

    3. A dashboard calls the lot with the highest VCN per cell the outlier to investigate first. The FDA recommends reporting VCN per CAR-positive cell. Would you accept that call, and what single question do you ask? Use the good CAR-T table.[data][22]

      Answer Do not accept it yet; ask: “What is the denominator?” CT-LOT-2024-007: 1.8 / 0.452 = 3.98 copies per CAR-positive cell. CT-LOT-2024-009: 3.1 / 0.520 = 5.96[data]. Per cell, the order from highest is 009 (3.1), 008 (2.4), 010 (2.0), 007 (1.8). Per CAR-positive cell it is 008 (6.20), 009 (5.96), 010 (4.82), 007 (3.98). The top two swap, because lot 008 has the lowest CAR-positive share (38.7%). In the messy table, CT-LOT-2024-009 gives 3.1 copies per µg of genomic DNA, and neither per-cell value can be derived from it[data][22]. So the call is wrong on the FDA’s denominator: lot 008, not 009, is highest.

    4. Count subjects and serious events in the good and messy AE extracts. What would a safety summary built on the messy table report?[data]

      Answer Good: three subjects (STUDY-001-101-0001, -101-0002, -102-0003) and one serious event (pneumonia, AESER Y)[data]. Messy: “101-0001” does not match “STUDY-001-101-0001”, so the summary counts four subjects, one more (33%) than the extract contains. “yes” is not the controlled value Y, so a filter on AESER = Y finds 0 serious events instead of 1[41, 42]. Row 2 has no AEDECOD, so it drops out of any count by MedDRA preferred term.

    5. In the messy batch table, how many results for LOT-2024-008 are out of specification, and by how much? How does the good table record them?[data]

      Answer Two. Water is 0.62% w/w against “not more than 0.5”: 0.62 - 0.5 = 0.12 percentage points, 0.12 / 0.5 = 24% above the limit, yet the flag says N[data]. The assay cell hides a first run of 93.8% label claim against 95.0 to 105.0: 95.0 - 93.8 = 1.2 points below the lower limit. The good table flags water as OOS under INV-2024-0011, and keeps assay run 1 (93.8, OOS) and the retest (101.0) as two rows under INV-2024-0012[data]. The first result stands unless a laboratory error is documented[54]. The batch cannot be released on the messy table.

    6. Using the good potency table, give the series average for CPD-0001 to CPD-0003 two ways: the arithmetic mean of IC50 and the IC50 from the mean pIC50.[data]

      Answer IC50s are 12.5, 480 and 3.1 nM[data]. Arithmetic mean: (12.5 + 480 + 3.1) / 3 = 165.2 nM. pIC50s are 7.9, 6.32 and 8.51: mean (7.9 + 6.32 + 8.51) / 3 = 7.58, and 10 to the power of minus 7.58 M is 26.5 nM[8]. The arithmetic mean is about six times higher because CPD-0002 dominates it. Report 26.5 nM, the geometric mean, and state the assay and its ATP level (10 µM), since IC50 values from other ATP levels are not comparable[5, 7].

    7. In the good vaccine table, which pair fails the lot consistency margin, and what does the messy table claim for it?[data]

      Answer Strain 2, Lot A / Lot C: the ratio is 1.24, but the 95% interval runs from 1.03 to 1.53, and 1.53 is above the 1.5 limit[data]. The guidance asks for the whole interval inside 0.67 to 1.5, so lot consistency is not shown for Strain 2[35]. The other five pairs pass. The messy table marks the failing pair Y, uses a wider 0.5 to 2.0 margin for one Strain 1 pair, and writes “Lot B vs Lot C”, where 0.93 for B / C would be 1 / 0.93 = 1.08 for C / B[data].

    8. A board asks what one Phase III entrant costs in Phase II spending. Use the mean out-of-pocket Phase 2 cost of $21.0 million, then two Phase II success rates: BIO’s 28.9%, and the 35.9% inside the same cost model.[1, 85]

      Answer On BIO’s rate: 1 / 0.289 = 3.46 Phase II programmes per entrant, and $21.0 million / 0.289 = about $72.7 million[85, 1]. On the rate inside the cost model (all therapy areas): 1 / 0.359 = 2.79 programmes, and $21.0 million / 0.359 = about $58.5 million[1]. Both are in 2018 dollars, out of pocket, with no cost of capital. The rate choice moves the answer by 24% (72.7 / 58.5 = 1.24), so quote the pair with their sources and years (2011-2020 for BIO, 2000-2018 for the cost model). The point for data: a wrong potency or exposure number that sends the wrong programme to Phase II spends, on this industry mean, about $21.0 million before anyone learns why.

    9. You are shown the messy mRNA table to support a go decision. How many of the five rows would you rely on as written, and what single question exposes the rest? From the good table, what share of the mRNA in lot MRNA-DP-2025-011 sits outside the particles?[data]

      Answer Ask: “Does each result have its method and pass its own criterion?” Good table: 94% is encapsulated, so 100 - 94 = 6% is outside the particles[data]. Messy table: 1 of 5 rows (row 1). Row 2 has no capping result but says it meets its criterion. Row 3 shows 6% against at least 90% and still says Y; it is probably the free share typed into the encapsulated column (100 - 6 = 94%), but the record cannot prove that. Row 4 gives a particle size with no method, and WHO asks that the method be stated because the result depends on it[33]. Row 5 gives integrity as 0.1 mg/mL, which is an amount; integrity is a share of the mRNA[33]. The drug product lot cannot be released on the messy table.
