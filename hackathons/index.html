Challenges and what I built

For each event: the brief, what I built, the numbers, and what broke.

  • NVIDIA-AWS BioAgentKG Challenge · 6 Oct – 17 Nov 2026 · solo TrialMindKG An agent that enriches, prunes and explains the Pharos drug-target graph, with 34 failed drug programmes as worked cases. Every number comes from a committed result file, including the test that failed. Read the write-up →
  • NeurIPS 2026 Virtual Embryo Challenge · Aug – Dec 2026 · solo (team Second Heart Field, agent track) Virtual Embryo Challenge data notes Two public data notes. E10.5 cells were sequenced about 40% shallower than E9.5 cells, and a model that continues the change between them carries that forward as if it were development. A scoring protocol reproduces 26 of 29 of my validation-board records to four decimal places. Read the write-up →
  • Built with Claude: Life Sciences hackathon · 7 – 13 Jul 2026 · team lead, team of 3 FACET One drug name in, four dossiers out, one each for a medicinal chemist, a pathologist, a molecular biologist and a computational biologist. The design rule is that retrieved values must appear unchanged. Read the write-up →
