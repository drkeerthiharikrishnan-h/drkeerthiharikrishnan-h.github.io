More than a decade at the bench on one question: how the matrix tells cells what to do. Fibulin-1 in the forming heart at MUSC; fibulin-1, EGFR and the cancer matrisome at IISER Pune. Since 2021, AI that helps research like that move faster.

Chapter 1 · 2008 – 2014 · Medical University of South Carolina
Cardiovascular development

Fibulin-1 holds the early heart's cells in line

Before the PhD I worked as a physician assistant in cardiac surgery at Madras Medical Mission, Chennai. My PhD in the Argraves lab, where fibulin-1 was discovered, asked what one matrix protein does for the forming heart. The answer was restraint: fibulin-1 limits endothelial-to-mesenchymal transition in the proximal outflow tract.

  • Fibulin-1-null embryos had hypercellular proximal outflow-tract cushions: 93% more cells at E9.5 and 43% more at E10.5.
  • At E9.5 the nulls had 58% more TGFβ2 and 27% more Snail1-positive nuclei.
  • Cushion explants from null embryos released 58% more migrating cells.
  • A second line of work showed fibulin-1 is needed for bone formation and Bmp-2-driven Osterix induction.

Chapter 2 · 2015 – 2021 · IISER Pune, Cell Adhesion Lab
Oncology · extracellular matrix

Fibulin-1 binds EGFR, and the matrix talks back to lung cancer cells

I joined Nagaraj Balasubramanian's lab as a postdoc in 2015 and won a DST Women Scientist grant in 2017 to work independently. The question moved from the embryo to the tumour: does the same matrix protein regulate a cancer cell's receptors?

  • Both fibulin-1 isoforms, FBLN1C and FBLN1D, bind EGFR whether or not it is activated. EGFR became the first known receptor for fibulin-1.
  • Knocking fibulin-1 down raised EGF-driven EGFR activation in Calu-1 lung cancer cells. Cells on knockdown-derived matrix adhered and spread more, and erlotinib reversed it.

Chapter 3 · 2021 – 2022 · IISER Pune → TCGA
Pan-cancer · computational biology

Across cancers, one matrisome network kept surfacing

With Srinivas Sheshagiri Prabhu I turned from the bench to The Cancer Genome Atlas. A pan-cancer analysis of matrisome genes in 30 cancer types identified CTHRC1 and a related network, including POSTN, MMP13 and SFRP4, as major ECM regulators across tumour types. It was the first project where the data, not the pipette, did the experiment. It is also where AI entered my work: as a way to ask a biologist's question of a dataset too large for a bench.

Chapter 4 · 2021 – present · Persistent Systems, then Thoughtworks
AI that accelerates research

Teaching models to read tissue, trials and papers

In 2021 I moved into industry so the biology could reach more labs than one. At Persistent Systems, first as Consultant for Oncology Solutions and then as Domain Expert in the Healthcare and Life Sciences Innovation Labs, and since 2024 as Industry Expert at Thoughtworks, the job has been translation: turn what a pathologist or a trial lead knows into something software can do, and prove it with evidence.

  • Computational pathology: classification and segmentation of colon cancer histology towards personalised medicine (medRxiv 2023), and a blog on moving pathology AI into clinical practice.
  • Smart Trials, a generative-AI accelerator for patient–trial matching, among six-plus accelerators. First hackathon with the team at Google Cloud India.
  • At Thoughtworks: an agentic platform for research teams, with AI safety guardrails for a biopharma client and golden datasets so answers are judged on evidence.
  • ISG Women in Digital Awards 2023, Silver Winner, Digital Innovator. Precision-medicine panel at the ISG SICE Summit 2023, Washington, D.C.
